# DENOMINATIVE VERBS

<!-- page 245 -->

The formation of primitive verbs ([372](../part-2-inflection/09-verbs/02-stems.md#s372)) is treated in 496-529, 607-624, 722-743.

##### <a id="s866"></a> §866

Denominative verbs are formed from the stems of nouns (substantives or adjectives). Verbs lacking such a noun-stem are made on the model of the ordinary denominative verb. The principal terminations are as follows:

\1. <span lang="grc"> **-αω** </span>: derived chiefly from words with <span lang="grc">ᾱ</span> stems (a few from words of the second declension). Verbs in <span lang="grc">-αω</span> denote _to do, to be_ , or _to have_, that which is expressed by the stem.

<span lang="grc">τῑμά-ω</span> _honour_ (<span lang="grc">τῑμή</span>, stem <span lang="grc">τῑμᾱ-</span>), <span lang="grc">ἀ̄ριστά-ω</span> _breakfast_ (<span lang="grc">ἄ̄ριστο-ν</span> _breakfast_), <span lang="grc">τολμά-ω</span> _dare_ (<span lang="grc">τόλμα</span> _daring_, stem <span lang="grc">τολμᾱ-</span>), <span lang="grc">κομά-ω</span> _wear long hair_ (<span lang="grc">κόμη</span> _hair_). <span lang="grc">κοιμά-ω</span>, _lull to sleep_, has no primitive noun.

On <span lang="grc">-ιαω</span> and <span lang="grc">-αω</span> denoting a desire or a bodily condition, see [868 b](#s868).

\2. <span lang="grc"> **-εω** </span>: derived chiefly from <span lang="grc">όε-</span> stems ([834 f](01-word-formation-introductory.md#s834)), and thence extended to all kinds of stems. Verbs in <span lang="grc">-εω</span> denote a _condition_ or an _activity_, and are often intransitive.

<span lang="grc">οἰκέ-ω</span> _dwell_ (<span lang="grc">οἶκο-ς</span> _house_, <span lang="grc">οἰκόε-</span>), <span lang="grc">φιλέ-ω</span> _love_ (<span lang="grc">φίλο-ς</span> _dear_, <span lang="grc">φιλόε-</span>), <span lang="grc">ὑπηρετέω</span> _serve_ (<span lang="grc">ὑπηρέτης</span> _servant_, <span lang="grc">ὑπηρετᾱ-</span>), <span lang="grc">εὐτυχ-έ-ω</span> _am fortunate_ (<span lang="grc">εὐτυχής</span> _fortunate_, <span lang="grc">εὐτυχεσ-</span>), <span lang="grc">μῑσ-έ-ω</span> _hate_ (<span lang="grc">μῖσος</span> _hate_, <span lang="grc">μῑσεσ-</span>), <span lang="grc">σωφρον-έ-ω</span> _am temperate_ (<span lang="grc">σώφρων</span>), <span lang="grc">μαρτυρ-έ-ω</span> _bear witness_ (<span lang="grc">μάρτυς, -υρ-ος</span>).

**a.** Some <span lang="grc">εω-</span>verbs from <span lang="grc">εσ-</span>stems have older forms in <span lang="grc">-είω</span> ([624 a](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s624)).

\3. <span lang="grc"> **-οω** </span>: chiefly derived from <span lang="grc">ο</span>-stems. Verbs in <span lang="grc">-οω</span> are usually factitive, denoting _to cause_ or _to make_.

<span lang="grc">δηλό-ω</span> _manifest_, _make clear_ (<span lang="grc">δῆλο-ς</span>), <span lang="grc">δουλό-ω</span> _enslave_ (<span lang="grc">δοῦλο-ς</span>), <span lang="grc">ζηλό-ω</span> _emulate_ (<span lang="grc">ζῆλο-ς</span> _emulation_), <span lang="grc">ζημιο´-ω</span> _punish_ (<span lang="grc">ζημίᾱ</span> _damage_), <span lang="grc">μαστῑγό-ω</span> _whip_ (<span lang="grc">μάστιξ, -ῑγος</span> _whip_). <span lang="grc">ἀρόω</span> _plough_ has no primitive.

On the formation of the present stem of verbs in <span lang="grc">-αω, -εω, -οω</span>, see [522](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/02-1-present-system.md#s522).

\4. <span lang="grc"> **-ευω** </span>: derived from substantives from <span lang="grc">ευ</span>-stems ([607](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s607)) and thence extended to other stems. <span lang="grc">ευω</span>-verbs usually denote a _condition_, sometimes an _activity_.

<span lang="grc">βασιλεύ-ω</span> _am king, rule_ (<span lang="grc">βασιλεύ-ς</span>), <span lang="grc">βουλεύ-ω</span> _counsel_ (<span lang="grc">βουλή</span>), <span lang="grc">κινδῡνεύ-ω</span> _venture_, _incur danger_ (<span lang="grc">κίνδῡνο-ς</span>), <span lang="grc">παιδεύ-ω</span> _educate_ (<span lang="grc">παῖς</span> _boy_, _girl_), <span lang="grc">θεραπεύ-ω</span> _attend_ (<span lang="grc">θεράπων</span> _attendant_).

\5. <span lang="grc"> **-ῡω** </span> (rare): from <span lang="grc">υ</span>-stems, as <span lang="grc">δακρύ̄-ω</span> _weep_ (<span lang="grc">δάκρυ</span> _tear_). Cp. [608](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s608).

\6. <span lang="grc">**-οζω**, **-ιζω**</span>: derived originally from stems in <span lang="grc">δ</span> or <span lang="grc">γ</span> (as <span lang="grc">ἐλπίζω</span> _hope_ = <span lang="grc">ἐλπιδ-ῖω, ἁρπάζω</span> _seize_ = <span lang="grc">ἁρπαγ-ιω</span>), and thence widely extended to other stems (cp. [623](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s623) <span lang="grc">δ, γ</span>). Such verbs denote _action_.

<span lang="grc">γυμνάζω</span> _exercise_ (<span lang="grc">γυμνάς, -άδ-ος</span> _stripped_, _naked_); <span lang="grc">ἀναγκάζω</span> _compel_ (<span lang="grc">ἀνάγκη</span> _necessity_); <span lang="grc">ἀτῑμάζω</span> _dishonour_ (<span lang="grc">ἄτῑμος</span>); <span lang="grc">βιάζομαι</span> _use force_ (<span lang="grc">βίᾱ</span> _force_); <span lang="grc">θαυμάζω</span> _wonder_ (<span lang="grc">θαῦμα</span>); <span lang="grc">φροντίζω</span> _take care_ (<span lang="grc">φροντίς</span>); <span lang="grc">ὑβρίζω</span> _insult_ (<span lang="grc">ὕβρι-ς</span> _outrage_); <span lang="grc">νομίζω</span> _consider_ (<span lang="grc">νόμο-ς</span> _custom_, _law_); <span lang="grc">τειχίζω</span> _fortify_ (<span lang="grc">τεῖχ-ος</span> _wall_, stem <span lang="grc">τειχεσ-</span>); <span lang="grc">χαρίζομαι</span> _do a favour_ (<span lang="grc">χάρις, -ιτος</span> _favour_). <!-- page 246 -->

**a.** Verbs in <span lang="grc">-ιζω</span> and <span lang="grc">-ιαζω</span> derived from proper names express an adoption of _language_, _manners_, _opinions_, or _politics:_

<span lang="grc">ἑλληνίζω</span> _speak Greek_ (<span lang="grc">Ἕλλην</span>), <span lang="grc">βακχιάζω</span> _act like a bacchante_ (<span lang="grc">βακχιάς</span>), <span lang="grc">λακωνίζω</span> _imitate Laconian manners_ (<span lang="grc">Λάκων</span>), <span lang="grc">μηδίζω</span> _side with the Medes_ (<span lang="grc">Μῆδος</span>).

**b.** Verbs in <span lang="grc">-εζω, -οζω</span>, and <span lang="grc">-υζω</span> are rare (<span lang="grc">πιέζω</span> _press_, poet. <span lang="grc">δεσπόζω</span> _am lord_, <span lang="grc">κοκκύζω</span> _cry cuckoo_).

\7. <span lang="grc"> **-αινω** </span>: originally from stems in <span lang="grc">-αν</span> + <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> ([518](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/02-1-present-system.md#s518)), but usually extended to other stems. See [620](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s620), III.

<span lang="grc">μελαίνω</span> _blacken_ (<span lang="grc">μέλᾱς</span> _black_, <span lang="grc">μελαν-</span>), <span lang="grc">εὐφραίνω</span> _gladden_ (<span lang="grc">εὔφρων</span> _glad_, <span lang="grc">εὐφρον-</span>), <span lang="grc">σημαίνω</span> _signify_ (<span lang="grc">σῆμα, σήματ-ος</span> _sign_), <span lang="grc">χαλεπαίνω</span> _am angry_ (<span lang="grc">χαλεπό-ς</span> _hard_, _angry_).

\8. <span lang="grc"> **-ῡνω** </span>: from stems in <span lang="grc">υν</span> + <span lang="grc">ῖω</span> ([519](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/02-1-present-system.md#s519)). The primitive words often show stems in <span lang="grc">υ</span>. See [620](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s620), III.

<span lang="grc">βαθύ̄νω</span> _deepen_ (<span lang="grc">βαθύ-ς</span> _deep_), <span lang="grc">ταχύ̄νω</span> _hasten_ (<span lang="grc">ταχύ-ς</span> _swift_), <span lang="grc">αἰσχύ̄νω</span> _disgrace_ (<span lang="grc">αἶσχ-ος</span> _shame_), <span lang="grc">θαρρύ̄νω</span> _encourage_ (<span lang="grc">θάρρ-ος</span> _courage_).

\9. On other denominatives in <span lang="grc">λω, νω, ρω</span>, see [620](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s620), III; on inceptives see [526](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/02-1-present-system.md#s526)-528.

\10. Parallel formations are frequent, often with different meanings.

<span lang="grc">ἀ̄ριστάω</span> _take a midday meal_, <span lang="grc">ἀ̄ριστίζω</span> _give a midday meal;_ <span lang="grc">ἀτιμάω</span>, (poet.) <span lang="grc">ἀτῑμόω, ἀτῑμάζω</span> _dishonour;_ <span lang="grc">δουλόω</span> _enslave_, <span lang="grc">δουλεύω</span> _am a slave;_ <span lang="grc">εὐδαιμονέω</span> _am happy_, <span lang="grc">εὐδαιμονίζω</span> _account happy, congratulate;_ <span lang="grc">θαρρέω</span> _am courageous_, <span lang="grc">θαρρύ̄νω</span> _encourage;_ <span lang="grc">ὁρκόω, ὁρκίζω</span> _make one swear an oath;_ <span lang="grc">ὁρμάω</span> _urge on_, <span lang="grc">ὁρμαίνω</span> (poet.) _ponder;_ <span lang="grc">ὁρμέω</span> _lie at anchor_, <span lang="grc">ὁρμίζω</span> _anchor_ trans. (<span lang="grc">ὅρμος</span> _anchorage_); <span lang="grc">πολεμέω</span> (<span lang="grc">πολεμίζω</span> Epic) _wage war_, <span lang="grc">πολεμόω</span> _make hostile;_ <span lang="grc">σκηνάω</span> _put in shelter_, mid. _take up one's abode_, <span lang="grc">σκηνέω</span> _am in camp_, <span lang="grc">σκηνόω</span> _encamp_, _go into quarters;_ <span lang="grc">σωφρονέω</span> _am temperate_, <span lang="grc">σωφρονίζω</span> _chasten;_ <span lang="grc">τυραννέω, τυραννεύω</span> _am absolute ruler_, <span lang="grc">τυραννίζω</span> _take the part of absolute ruler_, <span lang="grc">τυραννιάω</span> (late) _smack of tyranny_. Cp. [531](../part-2-inflection/10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/02-1-present-system.md#s531).

##### <a id="s867"></a> §867

**Frequentatives and Intensives**.—These are mostly poetical. <span lang="grc"> **-αω** </span> in <span lang="grc">στρωφάω</span> _turn constantly_ (<span lang="grc">στρέφω</span> _turn_), <span lang="grc">τρωχάω</span> _gallop_ (<span lang="grc">τρέχω</span> _run_), <span lang="grc">ποτάομαι, πωτάομαι</span>, and <span lang="grc">ποτέομαι</span>, _fly about_ (<span lang="grc">πέτομαι</span> _fly_). <span lang="grc"> **-στρεω** </span> in <span lang="grc">ἐλαστρέω</span> _drive_ (<span lang="grc">ἐλάω, ἐλαύνω</span>). <span lang="grc"> **-ταω** </span> in <span lang="grc">σκιρτάω</span> _spring_ (<span lang="grc">σκαίρω</span> _skip_). <span lang="grc"> **-ταζω** </span> in <span lang="grc">ἑλκυστάζω</span> _drag about_ (<span lang="grc">ἕλκω</span> _drag_). With reduplication, often with change of the stem-vowel, in <span lang="grc">ποιπνύω</span> _puff_ (<span lang="grc">πνέω</span> _breathe_, <span lang="grc">πνυ-</span>), <span lang="grc">πορφύ̄ρω</span> _gleam darkly_ (<span lang="grc">φύ̄ρω</span> _mix_), <span lang="grc">παμφαίνω</span> _shine brightly_ (<span lang="grc">φαίνω</span> _bring to light, make appear_).

##### <a id="s868"></a> §868

Desideratives express _desire_. Such verbs end in <span lang="grc">**-σειω**, **-ιαω**</span>, and rarely in <span lang="grc"> **-αω** </span>. Thus, <span lang="grc">πολεμησείω</span> _desire to wage war_ (<span lang="grc">πολεμέω</span>), <span lang="grc">ἀπαλλαξείω</span> _wish to get rid of_ (<span lang="grc">ἀλλάττω</span> _exchange_), <span lang="grc">γελασείω</span> _wish to laugh_ (<span lang="grc">γελάω</span>); <span lang="grc">στρατηγιάω</span> _wish to be general_ (<span lang="grc">στρατηγός</span>); <span lang="grc">φονάω</span> _wish to shed blood_ (<span lang="grc">φόνος</span> _murder_).

**a.** Verbs in <span lang="grc">-ιαω</span> and <span lang="grc">-αω</span> are formed from substantives. Those in <span lang="grc">-σειω</span> may come from the future stem.

**b.** <span lang="grc">-ιαω</span> and <span lang="grc">-αω</span> may denote a bodily affection: <span lang="grc">ὀφθαλμιάω</span> _suffer from ophthalmia_ (<span lang="grc">ὀφθαλμίᾱ</span>), <span lang="grc">βραγχάω</span> _am hoarse_ (<span lang="grc">βραγχός</span> _hoarse_). Some verbs in <span lang="grc">-ωττω</span> (<span lang="grc">-ωσσω</span>) have a similar meaning: <span lang="grc">τυφλώττω</span> _am blind_ (<span lang="grc">τυφλός</span>), and even <span lang="grc">λῑμώσσω</span> _am hungry_ (<span lang="grc">λῑμός</span> _hunger_).
