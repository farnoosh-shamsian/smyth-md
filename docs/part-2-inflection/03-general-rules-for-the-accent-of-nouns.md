# GENERAL RULES FOR THE ACCENT OF NOUNS

##### <a id="s205"></a> §205

Substantives and adjectives accent, in the oblique cases, the same syllable as is accented in the nominative, provided the ultima permits ([163](../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/07-accent-general-principles.md#s163)); otherwise the following syllable receives the accent.

1 decl. <span lang="grc">θάλαττα, θαλάττης, θαλάττῃ, θάλατταν, θάλατται</span> ([169](../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/07-accent-general-principles.md#s169)), <span lang="grc">θαλάτταις, θαλάττᾱς</span>.

2 decl. <span lang="grc">ἄνθρωπος, ἀνθρώπου, ἀνθρώπῳ, ἄνθρωπον, ἄνθρωποι</span> ([169](../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/07-accent-general-principles.md#s169)), <span lang="grc">ἀνθρώπων, ἀνθρώποις, ἀνθρώπους</span>.

3 decl. <span lang="grc">λέων, λέοντος, λέοντι, λέοντα, λέοντες, λεόντων</span>.

Adj.: <span lang="grc">ἄξιος</span> ([287](04-substantives/07-adjectives.md#s287)), <span lang="grc">ἀξίᾱ, ἄξιον, ἀξίου, ἀξίᾱς, ἀξίῳ, ἀξίᾳ, ἀξίων, ἀξίοις. χαρίεις</span> ([299](04-substantives/07-adjectives.md#s299)), <span lang="grc">χαρίεντος, χαρίεντι, χαρίεντα, χαριέντων</span>.

##### <a id="s206"></a> §206

The character of the accent depends on the general laws ([167](../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/07-accent-general-principles.md#s167), [168](../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/07-accent-general-principles.md#s168), [176](../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/07-accent-general-principles.md#s176)). Thus, <span lang="grc">νί̄κη, ϝῖκαι</span> ([169](../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/07-accent-general-principles.md#s169)); <span lang="grc">δῶρον, δώρου, δῶρα; σῶμα, σώματος, σωμάτων, σώματα</span>.

##### <a id="s207"></a> §207

Oxytones of the first and second declensions are perispomena in the genitive and dative of all numbers: <span lang="grc">σκιά̄, σκιᾶς, σκιᾷ, σκιῶν, σκιαῖς; θεός, θεοῦ, θεῷ, θεῶν, θεοῖς; φανερός, φανεροῦ, φανερῷ, φανερῶν, φανεροῖς</span>.

##### <a id="s208"></a> §208

The genitive plural of all substantives of the first declension has the circumflex on the <span lang="grc">ω</span> of <span lang="grc">-ων</span>. Thus, <span lang="grc">νί̄κη νῑκῶν; θάλαττα θαλαττῶν; πολί̄της πολῑτῶν; νεᾱνίᾱς νεᾱνιῶν</span>.

##### <a id="s209"></a> §209

The fem. gen. plural of adjectives and participles in <span lang="grc">-ος</span> has the same accent and form as the masculine and neuter. Thus, <span lang="grc">δίκαιος</span>, gen. pl. <span lang="grc">δικαίων</span> (in all genders); <span lang="grc">λυόμενος</span>, gen. pl. <span lang="grc">λυομένων</span> (in all genders). <!-- page 48 -->
