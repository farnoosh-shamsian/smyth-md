# VERBS: TENSE-SUFFIXES, THEMATIC VOWEL, MOOD-SUFFIXES

##### <a id="s455"></a> §455

**Tense-Suffixes**.—The tense-suffixes, which are added to the verb-stem to form the tense-stems, consist of the thematic vowel and certain other letters. No tense-suffixes are added to the verb-stem (1) in the second aorist active and middle, and second perfect and pluperfect, of <span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs; (2) in the perfect and pluperfect middle of verbs in <span lang="grc">-ω</span> and <span lang="grc">-μι</span>. The tense-suffixes are as follows:—

\1. Present system, <span lang="grc">-όε-, -τόε-, -\[ιγλιδε\]όε-, -νόε-, -ανόε-, -νεόε-, -να-, -νυ-, -</span>(<span lang="grc">ι</span>)<span lang="grc">σκόε</span>; or none, as in <span lang="grc">φα-μέν</span>.

\2. Future system, <span lang="grc">-σόε-</span>.

\3. First aorist sytem, <span lang="grc">-σα-</span>.

\4. Second aorist system, <span lang="grc">-όε-</span>; or none, as in <span lang="grc">ἔ-στη-ν</span>.

\5. First perfect system, <span lang="grc">-κα-</span> (plupf. <span lang="grc">-κη-</span> from <span lang="grc">-κεα-; -κει-</span> from <span lang="grc">-κεε-; -κε-</span>).

\6. Second perfect system, <span lang="grc">-α-</span> (plupf. <span lang="grc">-η-, -ει-</span>, or <span lang="grc">-ε-</span>); or none, as in <span lang="grc">ἕ-στα-τε</span>.

\7. Perfect middle system, none (future perfect <span lang="grc">-σόε-</span>).

\8. First passive system, <span lang="grc">θη-, -θε-</span> (future passive <span lang="grc">-θησόε-</span>).

\9. Second passive system, <span lang="grc">η, -ε-</span> (future passive <span lang="grc">-ησόε-</span>).

N.—<span lang="grc">-α</span> in the aorist is properly a relic of the personal ending ([666](../10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/13-inflection-of-omega-verbs.md#s666)).

For the Doric future <span lang="grc">-σεόε-</span>, see [540](../10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/03-ii-future-system.md#s540).—For the Epic first aorist <span lang="grc">-σόε-</span>, see [542 D](../10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/04-iii-first-sigmatic-aorist-system.md#s542).—For the doubling of <span lang="grc">ς</span> in the future and first aorist, see [534 b](../10-verb-stem-and-present-stem/03-ii-future-system.md#s534). D., 544 b. D.

##### <a id="s456"></a> §456

**Thematic Vowel**.—The thematic, or variable, vowel appears at the end of the tense-stems in the present, imperfect, and second aorist active and <!-- page 151 --> middle of <span lang="grc">ω-</span>verbs, and in all futures and future perfects. The thematic vowel in the indicative is <span lang="grc">ο</span> before <span lang="grc">μ</span> or <span lang="grc">ν</span> (and in the optative of the tenses mentioned); elsewhere it is <span lang="grc">ε</span>. Thus, <span lang="grc">λῡόε-, λιπόε-, λῡσόε-, λυθησόε-, λελῡσόε-; λύ̄ο-ῑ-μι</span>. In the subjunctive it is <span lang="grc">ώη</span>.

**a.** Attic inscriptions have both <span lang="grc">-εσθων</span> and <span lang="grc">-οσθων</span> in the imperative.

##### <a id="s457"></a> §457

**Subjunctive**.—In the subjunctive of all verbs the thematic vowel is <span lang="grc">ώη-</span>. Thus, <span lang="grc">λύ̄ω-μεν, λύ̄η-τε, λύ̄σω-μεν, στείλη-τε</span>.

**a.** Verbs in <span lang="grc">-νῡμι</span> form their subjunctive like <span lang="grc">ω-</span>verbs.

##### <a id="s457D"></a> §457 D

Hom. has <span lang="grc">-όε-</span> instead of <span lang="grc">-ώη-</span>, especially in the 1 aor., 2 aor. of <span lang="grc">μι-</span> verbs, and 2 aor. pass. (<span lang="grc">ἐρύσσομεν, δώομεν, τραπείομεν</span>; also in <span lang="grc">ἴομεν, εἴδομεν</span>). These forms do not occur in the sing. or 3 pl. active. Verbs in <span lang="grc">ω</span> rarely show this <span lang="grc">όε</span> in the present. (Other examples 532, 667 D., 682 D.)

##### <a id="s458"></a> §458

In the present and second aorist of <span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs, and in the aorist passive, <span lang="grc">ώη</span> is added to the tense stem. Thus <span lang="grc">τιθῶμεν</span> from <span lang="grc">τιθέ-ω-μεν, θῶ</span> from <span lang="grc">θέ-ω, τιθῆτε</span> from <span lang="grc">τιθέ-η-τε, λυθῶ</span> from <span lang="grc">λυθέ-ω</span>.

##### <a id="s459"></a> §459

**Suffix of the Optative**.—The optative adds the mood suffix <span lang="grc">-ῑ-</span>, or <span lang="grc">-ιη-</span> which contracts with the final vowel of the tense-stem: <span lang="grc">λύ̄οιμι</span> for <span lang="grc">λύ̄ο-ῑ-μι, φιλοίην</span> for <span lang="grc">φιλεο-ίη-ν, τιθείην</span> for <span lang="grc">τιθε-ίη-ν. -ιη-</span> occurs only before active endings. When the suffix is <span lang="grc">-ιη-</span>, the 1 pers. sing. ends in <span lang="grc">-ν</span>; as <span lang="grc">τῑμαο-ίη-ν ῀ τῑμῴην</span>; when it is <span lang="grc">-ῑ-</span>, the 1 pers. sing. ends in <span lang="grc">-μι</span>, as <span lang="grc">τῑμάο-ῑ-μι ῀ τῑμῷμι</span>.

##### <a id="s460"></a> §460

<span lang="grc"> **ιη** </span> is used as follows (in all other cases <span lang="grc">-ῑ-</span>):—

**a.** In contracted verbs in the singular, rarely in the dual and plural. <span lang="grc">-ῑ-</span> appears in the dual and plural, rarely in the singular.

**b.** In liquid verbs in the future active singular: <span lang="grc">φανοίη-ν</span> for <span lang="grc">φανεο-ίη-ν</span>. In the dual and plural <span lang="grc">-ῑ-</span>: <span lang="grc">φανοῖτον, φανοῖμεν</span> for <span lang="grc">φανεό-ῑ-τον, φανεό-ῑ-μεν</span>.

**c.** In the singular of <span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs: <span lang="grc">τιθείην</span> for <span lang="grc">τιθε-ίη-ν, διδοίην</span> for <span lang="grc">διδο-ίη-ν, θείην</span> for <span lang="grc">θε-ίη-ν</span>. Here the modal sign is added to the tense-stem without any thematic vowel. <span lang="grc">-ῑ-</span> is more common in the dual and plural: <span lang="grc">τιθεῖμεν</span> for <span lang="grc">τιθέ-ῑ-μεν, διδοῖμεν</span> for <span lang="grc">διδό-ῑ-μεν, θεῖτε</span> for <span lang="grc">θέ-ῑ-τε</span>. Verbs in <span lang="grc">-νῡμι</span> make their optatives like <span lang="grc">λύ̄ω</span>.

**d.** In the aorist passive: <span lang="grc">λυθείην</span> for <span lang="grc">λυθε-ίη-ν, φανείην</span> for <span lang="grc">φανε-ίη-ν</span>. In the dual and plural <span lang="grc">-ῑ-</span> is more common: <span lang="grc">λυθεῖμεν</span> for <span lang="grc">λυθέ-ῑ-μεν, φανεῖτε</span> for <span lang="grc">φανέ-ῑ-τε</span>.

**e.** In some second perfects, as <span lang="grc">προεληλυθοίης</span>, and in the second aorist <span lang="grc">σχοίην</span> from <span lang="grc">ἔχω</span> (but <span lang="grc">-σχοῖμι</span> in composition).

N.—In the 3 pl. <span lang="grc">-ιε-</span> is regular before <span lang="grc">-ν</span>: <span lang="grc">λύ̄ο-ιε-ν, τιθε-ῖε-ν, λυθε-ῖε-ν</span>.

##### <a id="s460D"></a> §460 D

<span lang="grc">-ιη-</span> is very rare in Hom. in the dual and plural.

##### <a id="s461"></a> §461

**a.** In the 1 aor. opt. act. of <span lang="grc">ω-</span>verbs the endings <span lang="grc">-ειας, -ειε</span>, and <span lang="grc">-ειαν</span> are more common than <span lang="grc">-αις, -αι, -αιεν</span>.

**b.** In the aor. opt. passive of all verbs and in the opt. of <span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs and of contract verbs <span lang="grc">-ιτον, -ιτην, -ιμεν, -ιτε, -ιεν</span> are commoner than <span lang="grc">-ιητον, -ιητην, -ιημεν, -ιητε, -ιησαν</span>. Prose writers use either the shorter or the longer forms; poets use only the shorter forms. Except in contract verbs <span lang="grc">-ιητε</span> is very common in the 2 pl. and is sometimes the only form in the Mss., as <span lang="grc">δοίητε, θει´ητε, γνοίητε, -βαίητε, λυθείητε, φανείητε</span>; but the forms in question occur in prose writers and their genuineness is therefore unsupported by metrical evidence.
