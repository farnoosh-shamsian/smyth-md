##### <a id="s235D"></a> §235 D

Homeric and Ionic generally have the open forms. <span lang="grc">οἰνοχόος</span> _winepourer_ does not contract in Attic since it stands for <span lang="grc">οἰνοχοϝος</span>.

##### <a id="s236"></a> §236

**Accent**.—**a.** The nominative dual is irregularly oxytone: <span lang="grc">νώ, ὀστώ</span>, not <span lang="grc">νῶ, ὀστῶ</span> according to 171, N. 2.

**b.** <span lang="grc">κανοῦν</span> (<span lang="grc">κάνεον</span>) _basket_ receives its accent (not <span lang="grc">κάνουν</span>) from that of the genitive and dative <span lang="grc">κανοῦ, κανῷ</span>. Cp. [290 c](07-adjectives.md#s290).

**c.** Compounds retain the accent on the syllable that has it in the nominative singular: <span lang="grc">ἔκπλους</span> from <span lang="grc">ἔκπλοος; ἔκπλου</span> (not <span lang="grc">ἐκπλοῦ</span>) from <span lang="grc">ἐκπλόου; ἔκπλων</span> (not <span lang="grc">ἐκπλῶν</span>) from <span lang="grc">ἐκπλόων</span>.
