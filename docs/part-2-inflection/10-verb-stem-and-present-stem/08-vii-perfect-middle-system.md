# VERB-STEM AND PRESENT STEM: VII. PERFECT MIDDLE SYSTEM

##### <a id="s574"></a> §574

The stem of the perfect and pluperfect middle and passive is the reduplicated verb-stem, to which the personal endings are directly attached. <span lang="grc">λέλυ-μαι</span> _I have loosed myself_ or _have been loosed_, <span lang="grc">ἐ-λελύ-μην; δέδο-μαι</span> (<span lang="grc">δί-δω-μι</span> _give_), <span lang="grc">δέδειγ-μαι</span> (<span lang="grc">δείκ-νῡ-μι</span> _show_). On the euphonic changes of consonants, see [409](../09-verbs/00-introduction.md#s409).

##### <a id="s574D"></a> §574 D

A thematic vowel precedes the ending in Hom. <span lang="grc">μέμβλεται</span> (<span lang="grc">μέλω</span> _care for_), <span lang="grc">ὀρώρεται</span> (<span lang="grc">ο᾽̓ρνυμι</span> _rouse_). <!-- page 179 -->

##### <a id="s575"></a> §575

The stem of the perfect middle is in general the same as that of the first perfect active as regards its vowel ([557](06-v-first-k-perfect-system.md#s557)), the retention or expulsion of <span lang="grc">ν</span> ([559 a](06-v-first-k-perfect-system.md#s559)), and metathesis ([559 d](06-v-first-k-perfect-system.md#s559)).

<span lang="grc">τῑμά-ω</span> _honour_ <span lang="grc">τετί̄μη-μαι ἐτετῑμήμην; ποιέ-ω</span> _make_ <span lang="grc">πεποίη-μαι ἐπεποιήμην; γράφ-ω</span> _write_ <span lang="grc">γέγραμ-μαι; κρί̄νω</span> (<span lang="grc">κριν-</span>) _judge_ <span lang="grc">κέκρι-μαι; τείνω</span> (<span lang="grc">τεν-</span>) _stretch_ <span lang="grc">τέταμαι; φθείρω</span> (<span lang="grc">φθερ-</span>) _corrupt_ <span lang="grc">ἔφθαρ-μαι; βάλλω</span> (<span lang="grc">βαλ-</span>) _throw_ <span lang="grc">βέβλη-μαι ἐβεβλήμην; πείθω</span> (<span lang="grc">πιθ-, πειθ-, ποιθ-</span>) _persuade_ <span lang="grc">πέπεισμαι ἐπεπείσμην</span>.

##### <a id="s576"></a> §576

The vowel of the perfect middle stem should show the weak form when there is variation between <span lang="grc">ε</span> (<span lang="grc">ει, ευ</span>): <span lang="grc">ο</span> (<span lang="grc">οι, ου</span>): <span lang="grc">α</span> (<span lang="grc">ι, υ</span>). The weak form in <span lang="grc">α</span> appears regularly in verbs containing a liquid ([479](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s479)): that in <span lang="grc">υ</span>, in <span lang="grc">πέπυσμαι</span> from <span lang="grc">πυνθάνομαι</span> (<span lang="grc">πυθ-, πευθ-</span>) _learn_, poet. <span lang="grc">ἔσσυμαι</span> _hasten_ from <span lang="grc">σεύω</span> (<span lang="grc">συ-, σευ-</span>) _urge_.

##### <a id="s577"></a> §577

The vowel of the present has often displaced the weak form, as in <span lang="grc">πέπλεγμαι</span> (<span lang="grc">πλέκ-ω</span> _weave_), <span lang="grc">λέλειμμαι</span> (<span lang="grc">λείπ-ω</span> _leave_), <span lang="grc">πέπεισμαι</span> (<span lang="grc">πείθ-ω</span> _persuade_), <span lang="grc">ἔζευγμαι</span> (<span lang="grc">ζεύγ-νῡ-μι</span> _yoke_).

##### <a id="s578"></a> §578

A final short vowel of the verb-stem is not lengthened in the verbs given in 488 a. <span lang="grc">ε</span> is added ([485](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s485)) in many verbs. For metathesis see [492](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s492); for Attic reduplication see [446](../09-verbs/06-reduplication.md#s446).

##### <a id="s579"></a> §579

<span lang="grc">ν</span> is retained in endings not beginning with <span lang="grc">μ</span>, as <span lang="grc">φαίνω</span> (<span lang="grc">φαν-</span>) _show_, <span lang="grc">πέφανται, πέφανθε</span>. Before <span lang="grc">-μαι</span>, we have <span lang="grc">μ</span> in <span lang="grc">ὤξυμμαι</span> from <span lang="grc">ὀξύ̄νω</span> (<span lang="grc">ὀξυν-</span>) _sharpen_, but usually <span lang="grc">ν</span> is replaced by <span lang="grc">ς</span>. On the insertion of <span lang="grc">ς</span>, see [489](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s489).

##### <a id="s580"></a> §580

**Future Perfect**.—The stem of the future perfect is formed by adding <span lang="grc">-σόε-</span> to the stem of the perfect middle. A vowel immediately preceding <span lang="grc">-σόε-</span> is always long, though it may have been short in the perfect middle.

<span lang="grc">λύ̄-ω</span> _loose_, <span lang="grc">λελύ̄-σομαι</span> _I shall have been loosed_ (perf. mid. <span lang="grc">λέλῠ-μαι</span>), <span lang="grc">δέ-ω</span> _bind_ <span lang="grc">δεδή-σομαι</span> (perf. mid. <span lang="grc">δέδε-μαι</span>), <span lang="grc">γράφ-ω</span> _write_ <span lang="grc">γεγράψ-ομαι, καλέω</span> _call_ <span lang="grc">κεκλήσομαι</span>.

##### <a id="s580D"></a> §580 D

Hom. has <span lang="grc">δεδέξουαι, μεμνήσομαι, κεκλήσῃ, κεχολώσεται; κεκαδήσομαι, πεφιδήσεται</span> are from reduplicated aorists.

##### <a id="s581"></a> §581

The future perfect usually has a passive force. The _active_ meaning is found where the perfect middle or active has an active meaning ([1946](../../part-4-syntax/20-the-verb-voices/10-the-tenses.md#s1946), [1947](../../part-4-syntax/20-the-verb-voices/10-the-tenses.md#s1947)).

<span lang="grc">κεκτήσομαι</span> _shall possess_ (<span lang="grc">κέκτημαι</span> _possess_), <span lang="grc">κεκρά̄ξομαι</span> _shall cry out_ (<span lang="grc">κέκρᾱγα</span> _cry out_), <span lang="grc">κεκλάγξομαι</span> _shall scream_ (<span lang="grc">κέκλαγγα</span> _scream_), <span lang="grc">μεμνήσομαι</span> _shall remember_ (<span lang="grc">μέμνημαι</span> _remember_), <span lang="grc">πεπαύσομαι</span> _shall have ceased_ (<span lang="grc">πέπαυμαι</span> _have ceased_).

##### <a id="s582"></a> §582

Not all verbs can form a future perfect; and few forms of this tense occur outside of the indicative: <span lang="grc">διαπεπολεμησόμενον</span> Thuc. 7. 25 is the only sure example of the participle in classical Greek. The infinitive <span lang="grc">μεμνήσεσθαι</span> occurs in Hom. and Attic prose.

##### <a id="s583"></a> §583

The periphrastic construction ([601](11-periphrastic-forms.md#s601)) of the perfect middle (passive) participle with <span lang="grc">ἔσομαι</span> may be used for the future perfect, as <span lang="grc">ἐψευσμένος ἔσομαι</span> _I shall have been deceived_. <!-- page 180 -->

##### <a id="s584"></a> §584

**Future Perfect Active**.—The future perfect active of most verbs is formed periphrastically ([600](11-periphrastic-forms.md#s600)). Two perfects with a present meaning, <span lang="grc">ἕστηκα</span> _I stand_ (<span lang="grc">ἵστημι</span> _set_) and <span lang="grc">τέθνηκα</span> _I am dead_ (<span lang="grc">θνῄσκω</span>), form the future perfects <span lang="grc">ἑστήξω</span> _I shall stand_, <span lang="grc">τεθνήξω</span> _I shall be dead_.

##### <a id="s584D"></a> §584 D

Hom. has <span lang="grc">κεχαρήσω</span> and <span lang="grc">κεχαρήσομαι</span> from <span lang="grc">χαίρω</span> (<span lang="grc">χαρ-</span>) _rejoice_.
