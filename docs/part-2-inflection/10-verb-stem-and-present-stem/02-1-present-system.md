# VERB-STEM AND PRESENT STEM: 1. PRESENT SYSTEM

## (PRESENT AND IMPERFECT ACTIVE AND MIDDLE)

##### <a id="s497"></a> §497

The present stem is formed from the verb-stem in five different ways. There are, therefore, five classes of present stems. The verb-stem is sometimes the present stem, but usually it is strengthened in different ways. A sixth class consists of irregular verbs, the present stem of which is not connected with the stem or stems of other tenses.

## FIRST OR SIMPLE CLASS

##### <a id="s498"></a> §498

Presents of the Simple Class are formed from the verb-stem with or without the thematic vowel.

##### <a id="s499"></a> §499

(I) Presents with the thematic vowel (<span lang="grc">ω-</span>verbs). The present stem is made by adding the thematic vowel <span lang="grc">όε-</span> to the verb-stem, as <span lang="grc">λύ̄-ω, παιδεύ-ω, παύ-ω, μέν-ω, πείθ-ω, φεύγ-ω</span>, and the denominative verbs <span lang="grc">τῑμά-ω, φιλέ-ω, βασιλεύ-ω</span>. For the personal endings, see [463](../09-verbs/08-endings-of-the-verb-personal-endings.md#s463) ff. For the derivation of many of these verbs, see [522](#s522).

##### <a id="s500"></a> §500

The final vowel of the verb-stem is long in the present indicative, but either long or short in the other tense-stems, of the following verbs in <span lang="grc">-υω</span> or <span lang="grc">-ιω</span>.

**1. a.** Verbs in <span lang="grc">-υω</span> generally have <span lang="grc">ῡ</span> in Attic in the present; as <span lang="grc">λύ̄ω</span> _loose_, <span lang="grc">δύ̄ω</span> _go under_, <span lang="grc">θύ̄ω</span> _sacrifice_ (almost always), <span lang="grc">φύ̄ω</span> _make grow_ (usually). Also in <span lang="grc">ἀλύ̄ω, ἀρτύ̄ω, βρενθύ̄ομαι, γηρύ̄ομαι, δακρύ̄ω</span> (once <span lang="grc">ῠ</span>), <span lang="grc">ἱδρύ̄ω, ἰσχύ̄ω, καττύ̄ω, κνύ̄ω,</span>, <!-- page 164 --> <span lang="grc">κωκύ̄ω, κωλύ̄ω</span> (usually), <span lang="grc">μηνύ̄ω, ὀπύ̄ω</span> (<span lang="grc">ὀπυίω</span>), <span lang="grc">πτύ̄ω, ῥύ̄ομαι, στύ̄ομαι, τρύ̄ω, ὕ̄ει</span>; possibly in <span lang="grc">εἰλύομαι, ἠμύω, μύω, ξύω, φλύω; ἐλινύω, μηρύομαι, πληθύω</span> (once <span lang="grc">ῠ</span>), <span lang="grc">φῑτύω. ὠρύω</span> (<span lang="grc">ῠ̄</span>) is doubtful.

**b.** <span lang="grc">-υω</span> has <span lang="grc">υ</span> short in <span lang="grc">ἀνύω, ἀρύω, βρύω, κλύω</span> (but <span lang="grc">κλῦθι</span>), <span lang="grc">μεθύω</span>, and in all verbs in <span lang="grc">-νυω</span>.

##### <a id="s5001D"></a> §500. 1. D

Homer has short <span lang="grc">υ</span> in <span lang="grc">ἀλύω, ἀνύω, βρύω, δύω, ἐρύω, ἠμύω, τανύω, φύω</span>, and in all denominative verbs except <span lang="grc">ἐρητύ̄οντο</span> and <span lang="grc">ἐπῑθύ̄ουσι</span>, where <span lang="grc">ῡ</span> is metrically necessary; long <span lang="grc">υ</span> in <span lang="grc">ξύ̄ω, πτύ̄ω, ὕ̄ω</span>; anceps in <span lang="grc">θύ̆ω</span> _sacrifice_ (<span lang="grc">ῡ</span> doubtful), <span lang="grc">θύ̄ω</span> _rush on, rage_, <span lang="grc">λύ̆ω</span> (rarely <span lang="grc">λύ̄ω</span>), <span lang="grc">ποιπνύω, ῥύομαι</span>. Pindar has <span lang="grc">υ</span> short in <span lang="grc">θύω</span> _sacrifice_, <span lang="grc">ἰσχύω, λύω, μανύω, ῥύω, ῥύομαι</span>, in presents in <span lang="grc">-νυω</span>, and in denominative verbs.

\2. Hom. has <span lang="grc">ῑ</span> in the primitives <span lang="grc">πί̄ομαι</span> and <span lang="grc">χρί̄ω</span>; but <span lang="grc">τί̆ω</span> and <span lang="grc">τί̄ω</span> (<span lang="grc">τείω</span>?); <span lang="grc">-ῐω</span> in denominatives (except <span lang="grc">μήνῑε</span> B [769](16-irregular-mi-verbs.md#s769)). <span lang="grc">κονί̄ω, ὀί̄ομαι</span> are from <span lang="grc">κονι</span>(<span lang="grc">σ</span>)<span lang="grc">-\[ιγλιδε\]ω, ὀι</span>(<span lang="grc">σ</span>)<span lang="grc">-\[ιγλιδε\]ομαι</span>.

\3. Where Attic has <span lang="grc">ῡ, ῑ</span> in the present, and Epic <span lang="grc">ῠ, ῐ</span>, the former are due to the influence of <span lang="grc">ῡ, ῑ</span> in the future and aorist.

\2. Attic has <span lang="grc">ῑ</span> in primitive verbs in <span lang="grc">-ιω</span>, as <span lang="grc">πρί̄ω, χρί̄ω, χλί̄ω</span>, but <span lang="grc">ῐ</span> in <span lang="grc">τίω</span>. Denominative verbs have <span lang="grc">ῑ</span>; but <span lang="grc">ἐσθί̆ω</span>.

##### <a id="s501"></a> §501

Several verbs with medial <span lang="grc">ῑ, ῡ</span> in the present, show <span lang="grc">ῐ</span> or <span lang="grc">ῑ, ῠ</span> or <span lang="grc">ῡ</span> in some other tense or tenses. Thus, <span lang="grc">θλί̄βω</span> _press_ <span lang="grc">τέθλιφα, πνί̄γω</span> _choke_ <span lang="grc">ἐπνίγην, τρί̄βω</span> _rub_ <span lang="grc">τέτριφα ἐτρίβην, τύ̄φω</span> _raise smoke_ <span lang="grc">ἐτύφην, ψύ̄χω</span> _cool_ <span lang="grc">ἐψύχην</span>.

##### <a id="s502"></a> §502

Verb-stems having the weak grades <span lang="grc">α, ι, υ</span>, show the strong grades <span lang="grc">η, ει, ευ</span> in the present; as <span lang="grc">τήκ-ω</span> (<span lang="grc">τᾰκ-</span>) _melt_, <span lang="grc">λείπω</span> (<span lang="grc">λιπ-</span>) _leave_, <span lang="grc">φεύγω</span> (<span lang="grc">φυγ-</span>) _flee_.

**a.** To this class belong also <span lang="grc">λήθω, σήπω, τέθηπα</span> _am astonished_, 2 aor. <span lang="grc">ἔταφον, ἀλείφω</span>, (<span lang="grc">δέδοικα</span>, [703](13-inflection-of-omega-verbs.md#s703)), <span lang="grc">εἴκω</span> (<span lang="grc">ἔοικα</span>), (<span lang="grc">εἴωθα</span>, 563 a), <span lang="grc">ἐρείκω, ἐρείπω, πείθω, στείβω, στείχω, φείδομαι; ἐρεύγομαι, κεύθω, πεύθομαι, τεύχω</span>.

##### <a id="s503"></a> §503

**Present Stems in <span lang="grc">-εο</span>**<span lang="grc">´ε-</span> **for <span lang="grc">ευ</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span>.—The strong form <span lang="grc">ευ</span> before the thematic vowel became <span lang="grc">εϝ</span> (<span lang="grc">ευ</span>) and then <span lang="grc">ε</span> ([20 a](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s20), [43](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s43)) in the verbs <span lang="grc"> **θέω** </span> _run_ <span lang="grc">θεύσομαι, **νέω**</span> _swim_ <span lang="grc">ἔνευσα, **πλέω**</span> _sail_ <span lang="grc">ἔπλευσα, **πνέω**</span> _breathe_ <span lang="grc">ἔπνευσα, **ῥέω**</span> _flow_ <span lang="grc">ῥεύσομαι, **χέω**</span> _pour_ <span lang="grc">κέχυκα, κέχυμαι, ἐχύθην</span>.

##### <a id="s503D"></a> §503 D

These verbs end in <span lang="grc">-ευω</span> in Aeolic (<span lang="grc">πνεύω</span> etc.). Epic <span lang="grc">πλείω, πνείω</span> have <span lang="grc">ει</span> by metrical lengthening ([28 D.](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s28)).

##### <a id="s504"></a> §504

(II) Presents without the thematic vowel (<span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs). The personal ending is added directly to the verb-stem, which is often reduplicated. The verb-stem shows different vowel grades, strong forms <span lang="grc">η, ω</span> in the singular, weak forms <span lang="grc">ε</span> (<span lang="grc">α</span>), <span lang="grc">ο</span> in the dual and plural. Thus <span lang="grc">τί-θη-μι, τί-θε-μεν; ἵ-στη-μι</span> for <span lang="grc">σι-στη-μι</span> (= <span lang="grc">σι-στᾱ-μι</span>), <span lang="grc">ἵ-στα-μεν; δί-δω-μι, δί-δο-μεν</span>.

**a.** All verbs in <span lang="grc">μι</span> (enumerated 723 ff) belong to this class except those in <span lang="grc">-νῡμι</span> ([523 f](#s523)) and <span lang="grc">-νημι</span> ([523 g](#s523)).

## SECOND OR **T** CLASS (VERBS IN <span lang="grc">-πτω</span>)

##### <a id="s505"></a> §505

The present stem is formed by adding <span lang="grc">-τόε-</span> to the verbstem, which ends in <span lang="grc">π, β</span>, or <span lang="grc">φ</span>. The verb-stem is ascertained from the second aorist (if there is one) or from a word from the same root. <!-- page 165 -->

| <span lang="grc">κόπτω</span> _cut_, | verb-stem <span lang="grc">κοπ-</span> | in 2 aor. pass. <span lang="grc">ἐ-κόπ-ην</span>. |
| <span lang="grc">βλάπτω</span> _injure_, | “ “ <span lang="grc">βλαβ-</span> | “ “ “ <span lang="grc">ἐ-βλάβ-ην</span>. |
| <span lang="grc">καλύπτω</span> _cover_, | “ “ <span lang="grc">καλυβ-</span> | “ <span lang="grc">καλύβ-η</span> _hut_. |
| <span lang="grc">ῥί̄πτω</span> _throw_, | “ “ <span lang="grc">ῥιφ-, ῥῑφ-</span> | “ 2 aor. pass. <span lang="grc">ἐ-ρρίφ-ην</span>. |

**a.** <span lang="grc">ἀστράπτω</span> _lighten_, <span lang="grc">χαλέπτω</span> _oppress_ may be from <span lang="grc">-π\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> ([117](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s117), [507](#s507)).

##### <a id="s506"></a> §506

Some of the verbs of this class add <span lang="grc">ε</span> in the present or other tenses, as <span lang="grc">ῥῑπτέω</span> _throw_, <span lang="grc">πεκτέω</span> _comb_, <span lang="grc">τύπτω</span> _strike_ <span lang="grc">τυπήσω</span>.

## THIRD OR IOTA CLASS

##### <a id="s507"></a> §507

The present stem is formed by adding <span lang="grc">-\[ιγλιδε\]όε-</span> to the verb-stem and by making the necessary euphonic changes ([109](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s109)-[116](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s116)).

### I. PRESENTS IN <span lang="grc">-ζω</span>

##### <a id="s508"></a> §508

**Dental Verb-stems**.—Verb-stems in <span lang="grc">δ</span> unite with <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> to form presents in <span lang="grc">-ζω</span> ([116](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s116)), as <span lang="grc">φράζω</span> _tell_ (<span lang="grc">φραδ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>), <span lang="grc">ἐλπίζω</span> _hope_ (<span lang="grc">ἐλπιδ-</span>), <span lang="grc">κομίζω</span> _carry_ (<span lang="grc">κομιδ-ή</span> _a carrying_), <span lang="grc">ὄζω</span> _smell_ (<span lang="grc">ὀδ-μή</span> _odour_), <span lang="grc">καθέζομαι</span> _seat myself_ (<span lang="grc">ἕδ-ος</span> _seat_).

**a.** <span lang="grc">σῴζω</span> _save_ (for <span lang="grc">σω-ιζω</span>) forms its tenses partly from the verb-stem <span lang="grc">σω-</span>, partly from the verb-stem <span lang="grc">σωι-</span>.

##### <a id="s508D"></a> §508 D

Aeolic has <span lang="grc">-σδω</span> for <span lang="grc">-ζω</span>.

##### <a id="s509"></a> §509

**Stems in <span lang="grc">γ</span>**.—Some verbs in <span lang="grc">-ζω</span> are derived from stems in <span lang="grc">γ</span> preceded by a vowel; as <span lang="grc">ἁρπάζω</span> _seize_ for <span lang="grc">ἁρπαγ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (cp. <span lang="grc">ἁρπαγ-ή</span> _seizure_), <span lang="grc">κρά̄ζω</span> _cry out_ (2 aor. <span lang="grc">ἔκραγον</span>). See [116](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s116), other examples 623 <span lang="grc"> **γ** </span> III.

**a.** <span lang="grc">νίζω</span> _wash_ makes its other tenses from the verb-stem <span lang="grc">νίβ-</span> (fut. <span lang="grc">νίψω</span>, cp. Hom. <span lang="grc">νίπτομαι</span>).

##### <a id="s510"></a> §510

A few verbs with stems in <span lang="grc"> **γγ** </span> lose one <span lang="grc">γ</span> and have presents in <span lang="grc">-ζω</span>; as <span lang="grc">κλάζω</span> _scream_ (<span lang="grc">κλαγγ-ή</span>), fut. <span lang="grc">κλάγξω; σαλπίζω</span> _sound the trumpet_ <span lang="grc">ἐσάλπιγξα</span> (also <span lang="grc">λύζω</span> _sob_, <span lang="grc">πλάζω</span> _cause to wander_).

##### <a id="s511"></a> §511

<span lang="grc">ῥεγ\[ιγλιδε\]ω, ἐργ\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> yield <span lang="grc">ῥέζω</span> _do_ (poetic) and <span lang="grc">ἔρδω</span> (Ionic and poetic). See [116](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s116).

##### <a id="s512"></a> §512

Most verbs in <span lang="grc">-ζω</span> are not formed from stems in <span lang="grc">δ</span> or <span lang="grc">γ</span>, but are due to analogy. See [516](#s516), [623](12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s623) <span lang="grc">γ</span> III, 866. 6.

### **II. PRESENTS IN <span lang="grc">-ττω</span>** (**IONIC AND LATER ATTIC <span lang="grc">-σσω</span>, 78**)

##### <a id="s513"></a> §513

**Palatal Verb-stems**.—Stems ending in <span lang="grc">κ</span> or <span lang="grc">χ</span> unite with <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> to form presents in <span lang="grc">-ττω</span> (<span lang="grc">-σσω</span>).

<span lang="grc">φυλάττω</span> _guard_ from <span lang="grc">φυλακ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (<span lang="grc">φυλακ-ή</span> _guard_ ([112](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s112))); <span lang="grc">κηρύ̄ττω</span> _proclaim_ from <span lang="grc">κηρῡκ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (<span lang="grc">κῆρυξ, κήρῡκ-ος</span>); <span lang="grc">ταράττω</span> _disturb_ from <span lang="grc">ταραχ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (<span lang="grc">ταραχ-ή</span> _confusion_).

**a.** <span lang="grc">πέττω</span> _cook_ is for <span lang="grc">πεκ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>; all other tenses are made from <span lang="grc">πεπ-</span>. <!-- page 166 -->

##### <a id="s514"></a> §514

Several verbs showing forms in <span lang="grc">γ</span> seem to unite <span lang="grc">γ</span> with <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> to form presents in <span lang="grc">-ττω</span> (<span lang="grc">-σσω</span>.) Thus <span lang="grc">ἀλλάττω</span> _change_, <span lang="grc">μάττω</span> _knead_, <span lang="grc">πλήττω</span> _strike_ (with the 2 aorists passive <span lang="grc">ἠλλάγ-ην, ἐμάγ-ην, ἐπλήγ-ην</span>), <span lang="grc">πρά̄ττω</span> _do_ (2 perf. <span lang="grc">πέπρᾱγα</span>, [571](07-vi-second-perfect-system.md#s571)), <span lang="grc">τάττω</span> _arrange_ (<span lang="grc">τᾱγ-ός</span> _commander_).

**a.** So <span lang="grc">δράττομαι</span> _grasp_, <span lang="grc">νάττω</span> _compress_ ([515 b](#s515)), <span lang="grc">νύττω</span> _push_, <span lang="grc">πτύσσω</span> _fold_, <span lang="grc">σάττω</span> _load_, <span lang="grc">σῡρίττω</span> _pipe_, <span lang="grc">σφάττω</span> _kill_, <span lang="grc">φράττω</span> _fence_. <span lang="grc">πρά̄ττω</span> has the late perf. <span lang="grc">πέπρᾱχα</span>.

##### <a id="s515"></a> §515

Some presents in <span lang="grc">-ττω</span> (<span lang="grc">-σσω</span>) are formed from stems in <span lang="grc">τ, θ</span> like those from <span lang="grc">κ, χ</span>.

Poet. <span lang="grc">ἐρέσσω</span> _row_ (<span lang="grc">ἐρέτ-ης</span> _rower_) aor. <span lang="grc">ἤρεσα</span>; poet. <span lang="grc">κορύσσω</span> _arm_ (<span lang="grc">κόρυς κόρυθ-ος</span> _helmet_), imperf. <span lang="grc">ἐκόρυσσε</span>.

**a.** So also <span lang="grc">βλίττω</span> _take honey_, <span lang="grc">πάττω</span> _sprinkle_, <span lang="grc">πτίττω</span> _pound_, and perhaps <span lang="grc">πλάττω</span> _form;_ also <span lang="grc">ἀφάσσω</span> Hdt., and poetic <span lang="grc">ἱ̄μάσσω, λαφύσσω, λίσσομαι</span>.

**b.** <span lang="grc">νάττω</span> _compress_ (<span lang="grc">ναγ-, ναδ-</span>) <span lang="grc">ἔναξα, νένασμαι</span> and <span lang="grc">νέναγμαι</span>. Cp. [514 a](#s514).

##### <a id="s516"></a> §516

**Formations by Analogy**.—a. As <span lang="grc">γ ¨ \[ιγλιδε\]</span> and <span lang="grc">δ ¨ \[ιγλιδε\]</span> unite to form <span lang="grc">ζ</span>, none of the verbs in <span lang="grc">-ττω</span> can be derived from <span lang="grc">-γ\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> or <span lang="grc">-δ\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>. Since the future and aorist of verbs in <span lang="grc">-ζω</span> might often seem to be derived from stems in <span lang="grc">κ, χ</span>, or <span lang="grc">τ, θ</span>, uncertainty arose as to these tenses: thus the future <span lang="grc">σφάξω</span> (<span lang="grc">σφαγ-σω</span>) from Epic <span lang="grc">σφάζω</span> _slay_ (<span lang="grc">σφαγ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) was confused in formation with <span lang="grc">φυλάξω</span> (<span lang="grc">φυλακ-σω</span>), and a present <span lang="grc">σφάττω</span> was constructed like <span lang="grc">φυλάττω</span>. Similarly, Attic <span lang="grc">ἁρπάσω</span> (<span lang="grc">-ομαι</span>) for Epic <span lang="grc">ἁρπάξω</span>; and so in place of (poetic) <span lang="grc">ἁρμόζω</span> _fit_ (<span lang="grc">ἁρμοδ-</span>) the form <span lang="grc">ἁρμόττω</span> was constructed.

##### <a id="s516D"></a> §516 D

Homer has many cases of this confusion; as <span lang="grc">πολεμίζω</span> (<span lang="grc">πολεμιδ-</span>) but <span lang="grc">πολεμίξω</span>. In Doric the <span lang="grc">ξ</span> forms from <span lang="grc">-ζω</span> verbs are especially common, as <span lang="grc">χωρίζω</span> _separate_, <span lang="grc">χωριξῶ, ἐχώριξα. παίζω</span> _sport_ has (late) <span lang="grc">ἔπαιξα</span>.

## III. LIQUID AND NASAL STEMS

##### <a id="s517"></a> §517

(I) Presents in <span lang="grc">-λλω</span> are formed from verb-stems in <span lang="grc">λ</span>, to which <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> is assimilated ([110](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s110)). Thus, <span lang="grc">ἀγγέλλω</span> _announce_ (<span lang="grc">ἀγγελ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>), <span lang="grc">στέλλω</span> _send_ (<span lang="grc">στελ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>).

##### <a id="s518"></a> §518

(II) Presents in <span lang="grc">-αινω</span> and <span lang="grc">-αιρω</span> are formed from verb-stems in <span lang="grc">-αν</span> and <span lang="grc">-αρ</span>, the <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> being thrown back to unite with the vowel of the verb-stem ([111](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s111)). Thus, <span lang="grc">φαίνω</span> _show_ (<span lang="grc">φαν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>), <span lang="grc">ὀνομαίνω</span> _name_ (<span lang="grc">ὀνομαν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>), <span lang="grc">χαίρω</span> _rejoice_ (<span lang="grc">χαρ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>).

**a.** Many verbs add <span lang="grc">-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> to the weak form of the stem, as <span lang="grc">ὀνομαίν-ω</span> for <span lang="grc">ὀνομαν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> from <span lang="grc">ὀνομṇ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>, cp. _nomen_ ([35 b](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s35)).

**b.** Hom. has <span lang="grc">κῡδαίνω</span> and <span lang="grc">κῡδάνω</span> _honour_, <span lang="grc">μελαίνω</span> _blacken_ and <span lang="grc">μελάνω</span> _grow black_. <span lang="grc">ὀλισθαίνω</span> _slip_ is late for <span lang="grc">ὀλισθάνω</span>.

**c.** The ending <span lang="grc">αινω</span> has been attached, by analogy, in <span lang="grc">θερμαίνω</span> _make hot_, etc. ([620](12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s620) III, 866.7). Likewise <span lang="grc">-ῡνω</span> ([519](#s519)) in poetic <span lang="grc">ἀρτύ̄νω</span> _prepare_, parallel to <span lang="grc">ἀρτύ̄ω</span> (in composition), by analogy to <span lang="grc">βαρύ̄νω</span> _weigh down_, <span lang="grc">ἡδύ̄νω</span> _sweeten_. <!-- page 167 -->

##### <a id="s519"></a> §519

(III) Presents in <span lang="grc">-εινω, -ειρω, -ῑνω, -ῑρω, -ῡνω</span>, and <span lang="grc">-ῑρω</span> are formed from stems in <span lang="grc">εν, ερ, ῐν, ῐρ, ῠν, ῠρ</span> with <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]όε-</span> added. Here <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> disappears and the vowel preceding <span lang="grc">ν</span> or <span lang="grc">ρ</span> is lengthened by compensation (<span lang="grc">ε</span> to <span lang="grc">ει; ι</span> to <span lang="grc">ῑ; υ</span> to <span lang="grc">ῡ</span>). See [37 a](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s37), [111](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s111).

<span lang="grc">τείνω</span> _stretch_ (<span lang="grc">τεν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>), <span lang="grc">φθείρω</span> _destroy_ (<span lang="grc">φθερ-</span>), <span lang="grc">κρί̄νω</span> (<span lang="grc">κριν-</span>), <span lang="grc">οἰκτί̄ρω</span> _pity_ (<span lang="grc">οἰκτιρ-</span>) generally written <span lang="grc">οἰκτείρω, ἀμύ̄νω</span> _ward off_ (<span lang="grc">ἀμυν-</span>), <span lang="grc">μαρτύ̄ρομαι</span> _call to witness_ (<span lang="grc">μαρτυρ-</span>).

**a.** <span lang="grc">ὀφείλω</span> (<span lang="grc">ὀφελ-</span>) _owe_, _am obliged_ is formed like <span lang="grc">τείνω, φθείρω</span> in order to distinguish it from <span lang="grc">ὀφέλλω</span> (<span lang="grc">ὀφελ-</span>) _increase_ formed regularly. Hom. has usually Aeolic <span lang="grc">ὀφέλλω</span> in the sense of <span lang="grc">ὀφείλω</span>. <span lang="grc">δείρω</span> _flay_ (<span lang="grc">δερ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) is parallel to <span lang="grc">δέρ-ω</span> ([499](#s499)).

##### <a id="s519D"></a> §519 D

Aeolic has here <span lang="grc">-εννω, -ερρω, -ιννω, -ιρρω, -υννω, -υρρω</span> ([37 D.](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s37) 3); for <span lang="grc">κτείνω</span>, it has <span lang="grc">κταίνω</span>; cp. Doric <span lang="grc">φθαίρω</span> for <span lang="grc">φθείρω</span>.

##### <a id="s520"></a> §520

**Verb-stems in <span lang="grc">-αυ-</span> for (<span lang="grc">α\[υγλιδε\], -αϜ-</span>)**.—Two verbs with verb-stems in <span lang="grc">-αυ</span> have presents in <span lang="grc">-αιω</span> from <span lang="grc">-αιϝω</span> out of <span lang="grc">-αϝ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> ([38 a](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s38)) : <span lang="grc"> **καίω** </span> _burn_ (<span lang="grc">καυ-, καϝ-</span>), fut. <span lang="grc">καύ-σω</span>; and <span lang="grc"> **κλαίω** </span> _weep_ (<span lang="grc">κλαυ-, κλαϝ-</span>), fut. <span lang="grc">κλαύ-σομαι</span>. Others 624 b.

**a.** Attic prose often has <span lang="grc">κά̄ω</span> and <span lang="grc">κλά̄ω</span>, derived from <span lang="grc">αιϝ</span> before <span lang="grc">ει</span> (<span lang="grc">κά̄εις</span>, and, with <span lang="grc">ᾱ</span> extended to the 1 person, <span lang="grc">κά̄ω</span>). Cp. [396](../09-verbs/00-introduction.md#s396).

##### <a id="s521"></a> §521

**Addition of <span lang="grc">ε</span>**.—The following verbs add <span lang="grc">ε</span> in one or more tense-stems other than the present: <span lang="grc">βάλλω</span> _throw_, <span lang="grc">καθίζω</span> _sit_, <span lang="grc">κλαίω</span> _weep_, <span lang="grc">ὄζω</span> _smell_, <span lang="grc">ὀφείλω</span> _owe_, _am obliged_, <span lang="grc">χαίρω</span> _rejoice_.

##### <a id="s522"></a> §522

**Contracted Verbs and Some Verbs in <span lang="grc">-ιω, -υω</span>**.—**a.** Verbs in <span lang="grc">-αω, -εω, -οω</span>, which for convenience have been treated under the first class, properly belong here, <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> (_y_) having been lost between vowels. Thus, <span lang="grc">τῑμάω</span> from <span lang="grc">τῑμα-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (<span lang="grc">τῑμᾱ-</span>), <span lang="grc">οἰκέω</span> _dwell_ from <span lang="grc">οἰκε-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (<span lang="grc">οἰκε-</span> alternate stem to <span lang="grc">οἰκο-</span>, 229 b), <span lang="grc">δηλόω</span> from <span lang="grc">δηλο-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>. So in denominatives, as poetic <span lang="grc">μηνίω</span> _am wroth_ (<span lang="grc">μηνι-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>), <span lang="grc">φῑτύ̄ω</span> _sow_ (<span lang="grc">φῑτυ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>). Primitives in <span lang="grc">-ῑω, -ῡω</span> are of uncertain origin. Cp. [608](12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s608), [624](12-first-conjugation-or-verbs-in-omega.md#s624).

N.—The rare spellings <span lang="grc">ἀλυίω, θυι´ω, μεθυίω, φυίω</span> indicate their origin from <span lang="grc">-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>.

**b.** So with stems in long vowels: <span lang="grc">δρῶ</span> _do_ from <span lang="grc">δρᾱ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω, ζῶ</span> _live_ from <span lang="grc">ζη-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (cp. <span lang="grc">ζῆθι</span>), <span lang="grc">χρῶ</span> _give oracles_ from <span lang="grc">χρη-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (2 pers. <span lang="grc">χρῇς</span>, [394](../09-verbs/00-introduction.md#s394)).

## FOURTH OR N CLASS

##### <a id="s523"></a> §523

The present stem of the N class is formed from the verbstem by the addition of a suffix containing <span lang="grc">ν</span>.

**a. <span lang="grc">-ν</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span> is added: <span lang="grc">δάκ-νω</span> _bite_, <span lang="grc">τέμ-νω</span> _cut_.

So <span lang="grc">δύ̄νω, κάμνω, πί̄νω, πίτνω</span> poet., <span lang="grc">τίνω, φθάνω, φθίνω</span>.

**b. <span lang="grc">-αν</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span> is added: <span lang="grc">αἰσθ-άν-ομαι</span> _perceive_, <span lang="grc">ἁμαρτ-άν-ω</span> _err_.

So <span lang="grc">αὐξάνω, βλαστάνω, δαρθάνω, ἀπεχθάνομαι, οἰδάνω, ὀλισθάνω, ὀφλισκάνω</span> ([526](#s526)).

**c. <span lang="grc">-αν</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span> is added and a nasal (<span lang="grc">μ, ν</span>, or <span lang="grc">γ</span> nasal) inserted in the verb-stem: <span lang="grc">λα-μ-β-άν-ω</span> (<span lang="grc">λαβ-</span>) _take_, <span lang="grc">λα-ν-θ-άν-ω</span> _escape notice_ (<span lang="grc">λαθ-</span>), <span lang="grc">τυ-γ-χ-άν-ω</span> _happen_ (<span lang="grc">τυχ-</span>).

So <span lang="grc">ἁνδάνω</span> _please_ (<span lang="grc">ἁδ-</span>), <span lang="grc">θιγγάνω</span> _touch_ (<span lang="grc">θιγ-</span>), <span lang="grc">κιγχάνω</span> _find_ (<span lang="grc">κιχ-</span>), <span lang="grc">λαγχάνω</span> _obtain by lot_ (<span lang="grc">λαχ-</span>), <span lang="grc">μανθάνω</span> _learn_ (<span lang="grc">μαθ-</span>), <span lang="grc">πυνθάνομαι</span> _inquire_ (<span lang="grc">πυθ-</span>).

**d. <span lang="grc">-νε</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span> is added: <span lang="grc">βῡ-νέ-ω</span> _stop up_ (also <span lang="grc">βύω</span>), <span lang="grc">ἱκ-νέ-ο-μαι</span> _come_ (also <span lang="grc">ἵ̄κω</span>), <!-- page 168 --> <span lang="grc">κυ-νέ-ω</span> _kiss_, <span lang="grc">ἀμπ-ισχ-νέ-ο-μαι</span> _have on_, <span lang="grc">ὑπ-ισχ-νέ-ο-μαι</span> _promise_ (cp. <span lang="grc">ἴ-σχ-ω</span> for <span lang="grc">σι-σχ-ω</span>, 493 a).

**e. <span lang="grc">-υν</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span> is added: <span lang="grc">ἐλαύνω</span> _drive_ for <span lang="grc">ἐλα-νυ-ω</span>.

**f. <span lang="grc">-νυ</span>** (<span lang="grc"> **-ννυ** </span> after a short vowel) is added (second class of <span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs, [414](../09-verbs/00-introduction.md#s414)): <span lang="grc">δείκ-νῡ-μι</span> _show_ (<span lang="grc">δεικ-</span>, present stem <span lang="grc">δεικνῡ-</span>), <span lang="grc">ζεύγ-νῡ-μι</span> _yoke_ (<span lang="grc">ζευγ-</span>), <span lang="grc">ὄλλῡμι</span> _destroy_ (for <span lang="grc">ὀλ-νῡμι</span>, 77 a); <span lang="grc">κερά-ννῡ-μι</span> _mix_ (<span lang="grc">κερα-</span>), <span lang="grc">σκεδά-ννῡ-μι</span> _scatter_ (<span lang="grc">σκεδα-</span>). Others 729 ff. Some of these verbs have presents in <span lang="grc">-υω</span> ([746](15-present-system.md#s746)).

N. 1.—The forms in <span lang="grc">-ννῡμι</span> spread from <span lang="grc">ἕννῡμι, σβέννῡμι</span>, which are derived from <span lang="grc">ἑσ-νῡμι, σβεσ-νῡμι</span>.

N. 2.—Some verbs in <span lang="grc">-νω</span> are formed from <span lang="grc">-νϝόε-</span> for <span lang="grc">-ν\[υγλιδε\]όε-</span>; as Hom. <span lang="grc">τί̄νω, φθί̄νω, φθά̄νω, ἄ̄νομαι</span> from <span lang="grc">τι-νϝ-ω</span>, etc., ([37 D.](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s37) 1). Attic <span lang="grc">τί̆νω</span>, etc. dropped the <span lang="grc">ϝ</span>.

**g. <span lang="grc">-να, -νη</span>** are added (third class of <span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs [412](../09-verbs/00-introduction.md#s412)); as in (poetic) <span lang="grc">δάμ-νη-μι</span> _I conquer_, <span lang="grc">δάμ-να-μεν</span> _we conquer_ (<span lang="grc">δαμ-</span>), and in <span lang="grc">σκίδ-νη-μι</span> (rare in prose for <span lang="grc">σκεδάννῡμι</span>) _scatter_. The verbs of this class are chiefly poetic (Epic), and most have alternative forms in <span lang="grc">-αω</span>. See [737](15-present-system.md#s737).

In two further divisions there is a transition to the Iota Class.

**h. <span lang="grc">-ιν</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span> for <span lang="grc">-ν-\[ιγλιδε\]όε</span> is added: <span lang="grc">βαίνω</span> _go_ (<span lang="grc">βα-ν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>), <span lang="grc">κερδαίνω</span> _gain_ (<span lang="grc">κερδα-ν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) <span lang="grc">τετραίνω</span> _bore_ (<span lang="grc">τετρα-ν-ιω</span>). So poetic <span lang="grc">ῥαίνω</span> _sprinkle_. For the added <span lang="grc">ν</span>, cp. <span lang="grc">δάκ-ν-ω</span> ([523 a](#s523)). See [518 a](#s518).

**i. <span lang="grc">-αιν</span>**<span lang="grc">όε-</span> for <span lang="grc">αν-\[ιγλιδε\]όε</span> is added: <span lang="grc">ὀσφραίνομαι</span> _smell_ (<span lang="grc">ὀσφραν-\[ιγλιδε\]ομαι</span>), Hom. <span lang="grc">ἀλιταίνομαι</span> _sin_ (also <span lang="grc">ἀλιτραίνω</span>). See [518 a](#s518).

##### <a id="s524"></a> §524

A short vowel of the verb-stem is lengthened in the case of some verbs to form one or more of the tense-stems other than the present. Thus, <span lang="grc">λαμβάνω</span> (<span lang="grc">λαβ-</span>) _take_ <span lang="grc">λήψομαι</span> (<span lang="grc">ληβ-</span>); <span lang="grc">δάκνω</span> (<span lang="grc">δακ-</span>) _bite_ <span lang="grc">δήξω</span> (<span lang="grc">δηκ-</span>). So <span lang="grc">λαγχάνω, λανθάνω, τυγχάνω, πυνθάνομαι</span> (<span lang="grc">πυθ-</span>) _inquire_, fut. <span lang="grc">πεύσομαι</span> (<span lang="grc">πευθ-</span>).

**a.** <span lang="grc">ζεύγνῡμι</span> _yoke_, <span lang="grc">πήγνῡμι</span> _fasten_, <span lang="grc">ῥήγνῡμι</span> _break_ have the strong grade in all tenses except the 2 pass. system. <span lang="grc">μείγνῡμι</span> _mix_ (commonly written <span lang="grc">μί̄γνῡμι</span>) has <span lang="grc">μῐγ-</span> only in the 2 perf. and 2 pass. systems.

##### <a id="s525"></a> §525

**Addition of <span lang="grc">ε</span> and <span lang="grc">ο</span>**.—**a.** Many verbs add <span lang="grc">ε</span> to the verb-stem to form all the tenses except present, 2 aorist, and 2 perfect; as <span lang="grc">αἰσθάνομαι, ἁμαρτάνω, ἁνδάνω, αὐξάνω, ἀπεχθάνομαι, βλαστάνω, δαρθάνω, κιγχάνω, μανθάνω, ὀλισθάνω, ὀφλισκάνω</span>. One or more tenses with <span lang="grc">ε</span> added are formed by <span lang="grc">κερδαίνω, ὄλλῡμι, ὀσφραίνομαι, στόρνῡμι, τυγχάνω</span>.

**b.** <span lang="grc">ὄμνῡμι</span> _swear_ has <span lang="grc">ὀμο-</span> in all systems except the present and future, as <span lang="grc">ὤμοσα, ὀμώμοκα</span>, but fut. <span lang="grc">ὀμοῦμαι</span> from <span lang="grc">ὀμεομαι</span>.

## FIFTH OR INCEPTIVE CLASS (VERBS IN <span lang="grc"> **-σκω** </span>)

##### <a id="s526"></a> §526

The present stem is formed by adding the suffix <span lang="grc">-σκόε-</span> to the verb-stem if it ends in a vowel; <span lang="grc">-ισκόε-</span> if it ends in a consonant. Thus, <span lang="grc">ἀρέ-σκω</span> _please_, <span lang="grc">εὑρ-ίσκω</span> _find_.

**a.** This class is called _inceptive_ (or _inchoative_) because some of the verbs belonging to it have the sense of _beginning_ or _becoming_ (cp. Lat. -_sco_); as <span lang="grc">γηράσκω</span> _grow old_. But very few verbs have this meaning.

**b.** In <span lang="grc">θνῄσκω</span> _die_, <span lang="grc">μιμνῄσκω</span> _remind_, <span lang="grc">-ισκω</span> was later added to verb-stems ending in a vowel. The older forms are <span lang="grc">θνήσκω, μιμνήσκω</span>. <!-- page 169 -->

**c.** The verb-stem is often reduplicated in the present; as <span lang="grc">γι-γνώ-σκω</span> _know_, <span lang="grc">βι-βρώ-σκω</span> _eat_, <span lang="grc">δι-δρά̄-σκω</span> _run away_. Poetic <span lang="grc">ἀρ-αρ-ίσκω</span> _fit_, poetic <span lang="grc">ἀπ-αφ-ίσκω</span> _deceive_, have the form of Attic reduplication. <span lang="grc">μίσγω</span> may stand for <span lang="grc">μι-</span>(<span lang="grc">μ</span>)<span lang="grc">σγω</span>.

##### <a id="s526cD"></a> §526 c. D

Hom. has <span lang="grc">ἐΐσκω</span> _liken_ for <span lang="grc">ϝεϝί</span>(<span lang="grc">κ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκω</span>, also <span lang="grc">ἴσκω</span> from <span lang="grc">ϝί</span>(<span lang="grc">κ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκω</span>, <span lang="grc">τιτύ</span>(<span lang="grc">κ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκομαι</span> _prepare_, <span lang="grc">δε-δί</span>(<span lang="grc">κ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκομαι</span> _welcome_.

**d.** A stop consonant is dropped before <span lang="grc">-σκω</span> ([99](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s99)); as <span lang="grc">δι-δά</span>(<span lang="grc">κ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκω</span> _teach_ (cp. <span lang="grc">δι-δακ-τός</span>), <span lang="grc">ἀλύ</span>(<span lang="grc">κ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκω</span> _avoid_, <span lang="grc">λά</span>(<span lang="grc">κ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκω</span> _speak_. <span lang="grc">πάσχω</span> _suffer_ is for <span lang="grc">πα</span>(<span lang="grc">θ</span>)<span lang="grc">-σκω</span> ([126](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s126)).

**e.** The present stem often shows the strong grades <span lang="grc">ω</span> (weak <span lang="grc">ο</span>) and <span lang="grc">ᾱ</span> or <span lang="grc">η</span> (weak <span lang="grc">α</span>). See b, c. Weak grades appear in <span lang="grc">φάσκω</span> _say_, <span lang="grc">βόσκω</span> _feed_.

**f.** On the iteratives in <span lang="grc">-σκω</span> see [495](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s495).

##### <a id="s527"></a> §527

The following verbs belong to this class (poetic and Ionic forms are starred):

## SIXTH OR MIXED CLASS

##### <a id="s529"></a> §529

This class includes some irregular verbs, one or more of whose tense-stems are quite different from others, as Eng. _am_, _was_, _be_, Lat. _sum_, _fui_. For the full list of forms see the List of Verbs.

\1. <span lang="grc">αἱρέω</span> (<span lang="grc">αἱρε-, ἑλ-</span>) _take_, fut. <span lang="grc">αἱρήσω, ᾕρηκα</span>, etc., 2 aor. <span lang="grc">εἷλον</span>.

\2. <span lang="grc">εἶδον</span> (<span lang="grc">ϝιδ-, ἰδ-</span>) _saw_, _vidi_, 2 aorist (with no present act.); 2 pf. <span lang="grc">οἶδα</span> _know_ ([794](16-irregular-mi-verbs.md#s794)). Middle <span lang="grc">εἴδομαι</span> (poetic). <span lang="grc">εἶδον</span> is used as 2 aor. of <span lang="grc">ὁράω</span> (see below).

\3. <span lang="grc">εἶπον</span> (<span lang="grc">εἰπ-, ἐρ-, ῥε-</span>) _spoke_, 2 aor. (no pres.); fut. (<span lang="grc">ἐρέω</span>) <span lang="grc">ἐρῶ</span>, perf. <span lang="grc">εἴ-ρη-κα, εἴρημαι</span>, aor. pass. <span lang="grc">ἐρρήθην</span>. The stem <span lang="grc">ἐρ-</span> is for <span lang="grc">ϝερ-</span>, seen in Lat. _ver-bum_. (Cp. [492](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s492).) <span lang="grc">ῥε-</span> is for <span lang="grc">ϝρε</span>, hence <span lang="grc">εἴρημαι</span> for <span lang="grc">ϝε-ϝρη-μαι</span>.

\4. <span lang="grc">ἔρχομαι</span> (<span lang="grc">ἐρχ-, ἐλευθ-, ἐλυθ-, ἐλθ-</span>), _go_. Fut. <span lang="grc">ἐλεύσομαι</span> (usually poet.), 2 perf. <span lang="grc">ἐλήλυθα</span>, 2 aor. <span lang="grc">ἦλθον</span>. The Attic future is <span lang="grc">εἶμι</span> _shall go_ ([774](16-irregular-mi-verbs.md#s774)). The imperf. and the moods of the pres. other than the indic. use the forms of <span lang="grc">εἶμι</span>. <!-- page 170 -->

\5. <span lang="grc">ἐσθίω</span> (<span lang="grc">ἐσθ-, ἐδ-, φαγ-</span>) _eat_, fut. <span lang="grc">ἔδομαι</span> ([541](03-ii-future-system.md#s541)), pf. <span lang="grc">ἐδήδοκα, -ἐδήδεσμαι, ἠδέσθην</span>, 2 aor. <span lang="grc">ἔφαγον</span>.

\6. <span lang="grc">ὁράω</span> (<span lang="grc">ὁρα-, ὀπ-, ϝιδ-</span>) _see_, fut. <span lang="grc">ὄψομαι</span>, perf. <span lang="grc">ἑώρᾱκα</span> or <span lang="grc">ἑόρᾱκα</span>, perf. mid. <span lang="grc">ἑώρᾱμαι</span> or <span lang="grc">ὦμμαι</span> (<span lang="grc">ὠπ-μαι</span>), <span lang="grc">ὤφθην</span>, 2 aor. <span lang="grc">εἶδον</span> (see [2](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s2) above).

\7. <span lang="grc">πάσχω</span> (<span lang="grc">παθ-, πενθ-</span>) _suffer_, fut. <span lang="grc">πείσομαι</span> for <span lang="grc">πενθ-σομαι</span> ([100](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s100)), 2 pf. <span lang="grc">πέπονθα</span>, 2 aor. <span lang="grc">ἔπαθον</span>. (See [526 d](#s526).)

\8. <span lang="grc">πί̄νω</span> (<span lang="grc">πι-, πο-</span>) _drink_, from <span lang="grc">πἱ̄-ν-ω</span> ([523 a](#s523)), fut. <span lang="grc">πί̄ομαι</span> ([541](03-ii-future-system.md#s541)), pf. <span lang="grc">πέπωκα</span>, 2 aor. <span lang="grc">ἔπιον</span>, imp. <span lang="grc">πῖθι</span> ([466](../09-verbs/08-endings-of-the-verb-personal-endings.md#s466). 1, a, [687](13-inflection-of-omega-verbs.md#s687)).

\9. <span lang="grc">τρέχω</span> (<span lang="grc">τρεχ-</span> for <span lang="grc">θρεχ-</span> ([125 g](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s125)), <span lang="grc">δραμ-, δραμε-</span>) _run_, fut. <span lang="grc">δραμοῦμαι</span>, pf. <span lang="grc">δεδράμηκα</span>, 2 aor. <span lang="grc">ἔδραμον</span>.

\10. <span lang="grc">φέρω</span> (<span lang="grc">φερ-, οἰ-, ἐνεκ-</span>, by reduplication and syncope <span lang="grc">ἐν-ενεκ</span> and <span lang="grc">ἐνεγκ-</span>) _bear;_ fut. <span lang="grc">οἴσω</span>, aor. <span lang="grc">ἤνεγκα</span>, perf. <span lang="grc">ἐν-ήνοχ-α</span> ([446](../09-verbs/06-reduplication.md#s446), [478](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s478)), <span lang="grc">ἐν-ήνεγ-μαι</span>, aor. pass. <span lang="grc">ἠνέχθην</span>.

\11. <span lang="grc">ὠνέομαι</span> (<span lang="grc">ὠνε-, πρια-</span>) _buy_, fut. <span lang="grc">ὠνήσομαι</span>, perf. <span lang="grc">ἐώνημαι, ἐωνήθην</span>. For <span lang="grc">ἐωνησάμην</span> the form <span lang="grc">ἐπριάμην</span> is used.

##### <a id="s530"></a> §530

Apart from the irregularities of Class VI, some verbs may, by the formation of the verb-stem, belong to more than one class, as <span lang="grc">βαίνω</span> (III, IV), <span lang="grc">ὀσφραίνομαι</span> (III, IV), <span lang="grc">ὀφλισκάνω</span> (IV, V).
