# VERB-STEM AND PRESENT STEM: VIII. FIRST PASSIVE SYSTEM (ΘΗ PASSIVE)

## FIRST AORIST PASSIVE

##### <a id="s585"></a> §585

The stem of the first aorist passive is formed by adding <span lang="grc">-θη-</span> (or <span lang="grc">-θε-</span>) directly to the verb-stem: <span lang="grc">ἐ-λύ-θη-ν</span> _I was loosed_, <span lang="grc">ἐ-φάν-θη-ν</span> _I was shown_ (<span lang="grc">φαίνω, φαν-</span>), <span lang="grc">ἐ-δό-θη-ν</span> _I was given_ (<span lang="grc">δίδωμι, δο-, δω-</span>).

**a.** <span lang="grc">-θη-</span> appears in the indicative, imperative (except the third plural), and infinitive; <span lang="grc">-θε-</span> appears in the other moods. <span lang="grc">-θη-</span> is found before a single consonant, <span lang="grc">-θε-</span> before two consonants or a vowel except in the nom. neuter of the participle.

##### <a id="s585aD"></a> §585 a. D

For <span lang="grc">-θησαν</span> we find <span lang="grc">-θεν</span> in Hom., as <span lang="grc">διέκριθεν</span>.

##### <a id="s586"></a> §586

The verb-stem agrees with that of the perfect middle herein:

**a.** Vowel verbs lengthen the final vowel of the verb-stem, as <span lang="grc">τε-τί̄μη-μαι, ἐ-τῑμή-θην</span>. On verbs which do not lengthen their final vowel, see [488](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s488).

**b.** Liquid stems of one syllable change <span lang="grc">ε</span> to <span lang="grc">α</span>, as <span lang="grc">τέ-τα-μαι, ἐ-τά-θην</span> (<span lang="grc">τείνω</span> _stretch_, <span lang="grc">τεν-</span>). But <span lang="grc">στρέφω</span> _turn_, <span lang="grc">τρέπω</span> _turn_, <span lang="grc">τρέφω</span> _nourish_ have <span lang="grc">ἐστρέφθην, ἐτρέφθην, ἐθρέφθην</span> (rare), though the perfect middles are <span lang="grc">ἔστραμμαι, τέτραμμαι, τέθραμμαι</span>.

##### <a id="s586bD"></a> §586 b. D

<span lang="grc">ἐστράφθην</span> is Ionic and Doric; Hom. and Hdt. have <span lang="grc">ἐτράφθην</span> from <span lang="grc">τρέπω</span>. Hom. has <span lang="grc">ἐτάρφθην</span> and <span lang="grc">ἐτέρφθην</span> from <span lang="grc">τέρπω</span> _gladden_.

**c.** Primitive verbs showing in their stems the gradations <span lang="grc">ε</span> (<span lang="grc">ει, ευ</span>): <span lang="grc">ο</span> (<span lang="grc">οι, ου</span>): <span lang="grc">α</span> (<span lang="grc">ι, υ</span>) have a strong form, as <span lang="grc">ἐτρέφθην</span> from <span lang="grc">τρέπω</span> (<span lang="grc">τρεπ-, τροπ-, τραπ-</span>) _turn_, <span lang="grc">ἐλείφθην</span> from <span lang="grc">λείπω</span> (<span lang="grc">λιπ-, λειπ-, λοιπ-</span>) _leave_, <span lang="grc">ἐπλεύσθην</span> from <span lang="grc">πλέω</span> (<span lang="grc">πλυ-, πλευ-</span>) _sail_.

**d.** Primitive verbs showing in their stems a variation between <span lang="grc">ε</span>: <span lang="grc">η</span> and <span lang="grc">ο</span>: <span lang="grc">ω</span> have, in the first aorist passive, the short vowel. Thus, <span lang="grc">τίθημι</span> (<span lang="grc">θε-, θη-</span>) <span lang="grc">ἐτέθην, δίδωμι</span> (<span lang="grc">δο-, δω-</span>) <span lang="grc">ἐδόθην</span>.

**e.** Final <span lang="grc">ν</span> is dropped in some verbs: <span lang="grc">κέ-κρι-μαι, ἐκρίθην</span>. See [491](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s491).

##### <a id="s586eD"></a> §586 e. D

Hom. has <span lang="grc">ἐκλίνθην</span> and <span lang="grc">ἐκλίθην, ἐκρίνθην</span> and <span lang="grc">ἐκρίθην; ἱδρύνθην</span> = Att. <span lang="grc">ἱδρύ̄θην</span> (<span lang="grc">ἱδρύω</span> _erect_), <span lang="grc">ἀμπνύνθην</span> (<span lang="grc">ἀναπνέω</span> _revive_).

**f.** The verb-stem may suffer metathesis: <span lang="grc">βέ-βλη-μαι, ἐ-βλή-θην</span>. See [492](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s492).

**g.** Sigma is often added: <span lang="grc">κε-κέλευσ-μαι, ἐ-κελεύσ-θην</span>. See [489](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s489).

##### <a id="s587"></a> §587

Before <span lang="grc">θ</span> of the suffix, <span lang="grc">π</span> and <span lang="grc">β</span> become <span lang="grc">φ; κ</span> and <span lang="grc">γ</span> become <span lang="grc">χ</span> ([82 c](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s82)); <span lang="grc">τ, δ, θ</span> become <span lang="grc">ς</span> ([83](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s83)). <span lang="grc">φ</span> and <span lang="grc">χ</span> remain unaltered.

<span lang="grc">λείπ-ω ἐλείφ-θην, βλάπτω</span> (<span lang="grc">βλαβ-</span>) <span lang="grc">ἐβλάφ-θην; φυλάττω</span> (<span lang="grc">φυλακ-</span>) <span lang="grc">ἐφυλάχ-θην, ἄγ-ω ἤχ-θην; κομίζω</span> (<span lang="grc">κομιδ-</span>) <span lang="grc">ἐκομίσ-θην, πείθ-ω ἐπείσ-θην; γράφ-ω ἐγράφ-θην, ταράττω</span> (<span lang="grc">ταραχ-</span>) <span lang="grc">ἐταράχ-θην</span>. <!-- page 181 -->

##### <a id="s588"></a> §588

<span lang="grc">θ</span> of the verb-stem becomes <span lang="grc">τ</span> in <span lang="grc">ἐ-τέ-θην</span>, and in <span lang="grc">ἐ-τύ-θην</span> for <span lang="grc">ἐ-θυ-θην</span> from <span lang="grc">τίθημι</span> (<span lang="grc">θε-, θη-</span>) _place_ and <span lang="grc">θύ̄ω</span> (<span lang="grc">θυ-, θῡ-</span>) _sacrifice_. See [125 c](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/03-euphony-of-consonants.md#s125).

## FIRST FUTURE PASSIVE

##### <a id="s589"></a> §589

The stem of the first future passive is formed by adding <span lang="grc">-σόε-</span> to the stem of the first aorist passive. It ends in <span lang="grc">-θησομαι</span>. Thus, <span lang="grc">παιδευθή-σομαι</span> _I shall be educated_, <span lang="grc">λυθή-σομαι</span> _I shall be loosed_.

<span lang="grc">τῑμάω, ἐτῑμήθην τῑμηθήσομαι; ἐάω, εἰά̄θην ἐᾱθήσομαι; λείπω, ἐλείφθην λειφθήσομαι; πείθω, ἐπείσθην πεισθήσομαι; τείνω, ἐτάθην ταθήσομαι; τάττω, ἐτάχθην ταχθήσομαι; τίθημι, ἐτέθην τεθήσομαι; δίδωμι, ἐδόθην δοθήσομαι; δείκνῡμι, ἐδείχθην δειχθήσομαι</span>.

##### <a id="s589D"></a> §589 D

Hom. has no example of the first future passive. To express the idea of the passive future the future middle is used. See [802](16-irregular-mi-verbs.md#s802). Doric shows the active endings in both futures passive: <span lang="grc">δειχθησοῦντι, ἀναγραφησεῖ</span>.
