# VERB-STEM AND PRESENT STEM: SECOND CONJUGATION OR VERBS IN MI

##### <a id="s717"></a> §717

Verbs in <span lang="grc">-μι</span> usually have no thematic vowel between the tense-stem and the personal endings in the present system (except in the subjunctive). The name “<span lang="grc">μι-</span>conjugation,” or “non-thematic” conjugation,” is applied to all verbs which form the present and imperfect without the thematic vowel.

##### <a id="s718"></a> §718

Of verbs ending in <span lang="grc">-μι</span> the following tenses are inflected according to the <span lang="grc">μι-</span>conjugation (except in the subjunctive): all non-thematic presents and imperfects; all aorists passive; all perfects and pluperfects middle; those second aorists active and middle in which the tense-stem does not end with the thematic vowel; one verb (<span lang="grc">ἵστημι</span>) in the second perfect and pluperfect active.

##### <a id="s719"></a> §719

Certain tenses of verbs ending in <span lang="grc">-μι</span> in the first person present indicative active, or in <span lang="grc">-μαι</span> in the present middle (and passive) when not preceded by the thematic vowel, are inflected according to the <span lang="grc">ω-</span>conjugation. These tenses are: all futures, all first aorists active and middle, most perfects and pluperfects active, and all subjunctives. Verbs in <span lang="grc">-νῡμι</span> regularly inflect the subjunctive and the optative according to the <span lang="grc">ω-</span>conjugation. Furthermore, the 2 sing. in the present and 2 and 3 sing. in the imperfect active of certain verbs, and some other forms, follow the <span lang="grc">ω-</span>conjugation ([746](15-present-system.md#s746)).

##### <a id="s720"></a> §720

Verbs in <span lang="grc">-μι</span> add the endings directly either to the verb-stem (here a root) or after the suffixes <span lang="grc">νυ</span> or <span lang="grc">νη</span>. Hence three classes are to be distinguished. <!-- page 203 -->

A. Root class; as <span lang="grc">φη-μί</span> _say_, verb-stem (and root) <span lang="grc">φα-, φη-</span>. This class often shows reduplication in the present and imperfect, as <span lang="grc">δίδω-μι</span> _give_.

N.—Two verbs have verb-stems ending in a consonant: <span lang="grc">εἰμί</span> _am_ (<span lang="grc">ἐσ-μι</span>) and <span lang="grc">ἧμαι</span> _sit_ (<span lang="grc">ἡσ-μαι</span>).

B. <span lang="grc">-νυ-</span> class; as <span lang="grc">δείκ-νῡ-μι</span> _show_, verb-stem <span lang="grc">δεικ-</span>, present stem <span lang="grc">δεικνῠ̄-</span>.

C. A few verbs, mainly poetical, add <span lang="grc">να-, νη-</span>; as <span lang="grc">σκίδ-νη-μι σκίδ-ναμεν</span> _scatter_, <span lang="grc">δάμ-νη-μι δάμ-να-μεν</span>, _subdue_.

##### <a id="s721"></a> §721

Deponent verbs without the thematic vowel are inflected according to the <span lang="grc">μι-</span>conjugation.
