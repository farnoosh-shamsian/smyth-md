# VERB-STEM AND PRESENT STEM: FIRST CONJUGATION OR VERBS IN Ω

##### <a id="s602"></a> §602

Verbs in <span lang="grc">-ω</span> have the thematic vowel <span lang="grc">-όε</span> (<span lang="grc">-ώη-</span>) between the tense-stem and the personal endings in the present system. The name “<span lang="grc">ω-</span>conjugation,” or “thematic conjugation,” is applied to all verbs which form the present and imperfect with the thematic vowel.

##### <a id="s603"></a> §603

Inflected according to the <span lang="grc">ω-</span>conjugation are all thematic presents and imperfects; those second aorists active and middle in which the tense-stem ends with the thematic vowel; all futures, all first aorists active and middle; and most perfects and pluperfects active.

##### <a id="s604"></a> §604

Certain tenses of verbs ending in <span lang="grc">-ω</span> in the first person present indicative active, or of deponent verbs in which the personal endings are preceded by the thematic vowel, are inflected without the thematic vowel, herein agreeing with <span lang="grc">μι-</span>verbs. These tenses are: all aorists passive; all perfects and pluperfects middle and passive; a few second perfects and pluperfects active; and those second aorists active and middle in which the tense-stem does not end with the thematic vowel. But all subjunctives are thematic. <!-- page 184 -->

##### <a id="s605"></a> §605

Verbs in <span lang="grc">-ω</span> fall into two main classes, distinguished by the last letter of the verb-stem:

\1. Vowel verbs: a. Uncontracted verbs. b. Contracted verbs.

\2. Consonant verbs: a. Liquid verbs. b. Stop (or mute) verbs.

N. Under 2 fall also (c) those verbs whose stems ended in <span lang="grc">ς</span> or <span lang="grc">ϝ</span> ([624](#s624)).

##### <a id="s606"></a> §606

**Vowel Verbs**.—Vowel verbs usually do not form second aorists, second perfects, and second futures in the passive. A vowel short in the present is commonly lengthened in the other tenses. Vowel verbs belong to the first class of present stems ([498](02-1-present-system.md#s498)-504; but see [612](#s612)).

##### <a id="s607"></a> §607

**Vowel Verbs not contracted**.—Vowel verbs not contracted have verb-stems ending in <span lang="grc">ῐ̄, ῠ̄</span>, or in a diphthong (<span lang="grc">**αι**, **ει**, **αυ**, **ευ**, **ου**</span>).

(<span lang="grc"> **ι** </span>) <span lang="grc">ἐσθίω</span> _eat_, <span lang="grc">πρί̄ω</span> _saw_, <span lang="grc">χρί̄ω</span> _anoint_, poet. <span lang="grc">δίω</span> _fear_, <span lang="grc">τί̄ω</span> _honour_ ([500](02-1-present-system.md#s500). 2); (<span lang="grc"> **υ** </span>) <span lang="grc">ἀνύω</span> _accomplish_, <span lang="grc">μεθύω</span> _am intoxicated_, <span lang="grc">λύ̄ω</span> _loose_, <span lang="grc">θύ̄ω</span> _sacrifice_, <span lang="grc">φύ̄ω</span> _produce_, <span lang="grc">κωλύ̄ω</span> _hinder_ (and many others, 500. 1 a); (<span lang="grc"> **αι** </span>) <span lang="grc">κναίω</span> _scratch_, <span lang="grc">παίω</span> _strike_, <span lang="grc">πταίω</span> _stumble_, <span lang="grc">παλαίω</span> _wrestle_, <span lang="grc">ἀγαίομαι</span> _am indignant_, <span lang="grc">δαίω</span> _kindle_, <span lang="grc">δαίομαι</span> _divide_, <span lang="grc">λιλαίομαι</span> _desire eagerly_, poet. <span lang="grc">μαίομαι</span> _desire_, <span lang="grc">ναίω</span> _dwell_, <span lang="grc">ῥαίω</span> _strike_; (<span lang="grc"> **ει** </span>) <span lang="grc">κλήω</span> (later <span lang="grc">κλείω</span>) _shut_, <span lang="grc">σείω</span> _shake_, Epic <span lang="grc">κείω</span> _split_ and _rest;_ (<span lang="grc"> **αυ** </span>) <span lang="grc">αὕω</span> _kindle_, <span lang="grc">θραύω</span> _break_, <span lang="grc">ἀπολαύω</span> _enjoy_, <span lang="grc">παύω</span> _make cease_ (<span lang="grc">παύομαι</span> _cease_), poet. <span lang="grc">ἰαύω</span> _rest;_ (<span lang="grc"> **ευ** </span>) <span lang="grc">βασιλεύω</span> _am king_, <span lang="grc">βουλεύω</span> _consult_ (<span lang="grc">βουλεύομαι</span> _deliberate_), <span lang="grc">θηρεύω</span> _hunt_, <span lang="grc">κελεύω</span> _order_, <span lang="grc">λεύω</span> _stone_, <span lang="grc">παιδεύω</span> _educate_, <span lang="grc">χορεύω</span> _dance_, <span lang="grc">φονεύω</span> _slay_. Most verbs in <span lang="grc">-ευω</span> are either denominatives, as <span lang="grc">βασιλεύω</span> from <span lang="grc">βασιλεύς</span>; or are due to the analogy of such denominatives, as <span lang="grc">παιδεύω. γεύομαι</span> _taste_ is a primitive. <span lang="grc">θέω</span> _run_, <span lang="grc">νέω</span> _swim_, <span lang="grc">πλέω</span> _sail_, <span lang="grc">πνέω</span> _breathe_, <span lang="grc">ῥέω</span> _flow_, <span lang="grc">χέω</span> _pour_ have forms in <span lang="grc">ευ, υ</span>; cp. poet. <span lang="grc">σεύω</span> _urge_, <span lang="grc">ἀλεύω</span> _avert_, <span lang="grc">ἀχεύω</span> _am grieved;_ (<span lang="grc"> **ου** </span>) <span lang="grc">ἀκούω</span> _hear_, <span lang="grc">κολούω</span> _dock_, <span lang="grc">κρούω</span> _beat_, <span lang="grc">λούω</span> _wash_.

##### <a id="s608"></a> §608

Some primitive vowel verbs in <span lang="grc">-ιω, -υω</span> ([522](02-1-present-system.md#s522)) formed their present stem by the aid of the suffix <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> (_y_), which has been lost. Denominatives in <span lang="grc">-ιω, -υω, -ευω</span> regularly added the suffix, as poet. <span lang="grc">μηνί-ω</span> _am wroth_ from <span lang="grc">μηνι-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span> (<span lang="grc">μῆνι-ς</span> _wrath_), poet. <span lang="grc">δακρύ̄ω</span> _weep_ (<span lang="grc">δάκρυ</span> _tear_), poet. <span lang="grc">φῑτύ̄-ω</span> _beget_ from <span lang="grc">φῑτυ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω, μεθύω</span> _am drunk_, <span lang="grc">βασιλεύω</span> _am king_. Poet. <span lang="grc">δηρί̄ομαι, μαστίω, μητίομαι, κηκίω, ἀχλύω, γηρύω, ἰθύω</span>.

##### <a id="s609"></a> §609

The stem of some of the uncontracted vowel verbs originally ended in <span lang="grc">ς</span> or <span lang="grc">ϝ</span> ([624](#s624)).

##### <a id="s610"></a> §610

Some verbs with verb-stems in vowels form presents in <span lang="grc">-νω</span> ([523](02-1-present-system.md#s523)), as <span lang="grc">πί̄νω</span> _drink_, <span lang="grc">φθί̄νω</span> _perish;_ and in <span lang="grc">-σκω</span> ([526](02-1-present-system.md#s526)).

##### <a id="s611"></a> §611

**Vowel Verbs contracted**.—Vowel verbs that contract have verb-stems ending in <span lang="grc">α, ε, ο</span>, with some in <span lang="grc">ᾱ, η, ω</span>.

##### <a id="s612"></a> §612

All contracted verbs form their present stem by the help of the suffix <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> (_y_), and properly belong to the Third Class ([522](02-1-present-system.md#s522)).

##### <a id="s613"></a> §613

Some contracted verbs have verb-stems which originally ended in <span lang="grc">ς</span> or <span lang="grc">ϝ</span> ([624](#s624)). <!-- page 185 -->

##### <a id="s614"></a> §614

**Liquid Verbs**.—Liquid verbs have verb-stems in <span lang="grc">λ, μ, ν, ρ</span>.

The present is rarely formed from the simple verb-stem, as in <span lang="grc">μέν-ω</span> _remain;_ ordinarily the suffix <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> (_y_) is added, as in <span lang="grc">στέλλω</span> (<span lang="grc">στελ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) _send_, <span lang="grc">κρί̄νω</span> (<span lang="grc">κρῐν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) _judge_, <span lang="grc">κτείνω</span> (<span lang="grc">κτεν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) _slay_, <span lang="grc">φαίνω</span> (<span lang="grc">φαν-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) _show_.

##### <a id="s614D"></a> §614 D

<span lang="grc">πεφύρσεσθαι</span> in Pindar is made from <span lang="grc">φύρσω</span> (<span lang="grc">φύ̄ρω</span> _knead_).

##### <a id="s615"></a> §615

A short vowel of the verb-stem remains short in the future but is lengthened in the first aorist ([544](04-iii-first-sigmatic-aorist-system.md#s544)). Thus:

**a. <span lang="grc">α</span>** in the future, <span lang="grc"> **η** </span> in the aorist: <span lang="grc">φαίνω</span> (<span lang="grc">φαν-</span>) _show_, <span lang="grc">φανῶ, ἔφηνα</span>. In this class fall all verbs in <span lang="grc">-αινω, -αιρω, -αλλω</span>.

**b. <span lang="grc">ε</span>** in the future, <span lang="grc"> **ει** </span> in the aorist: <span lang="grc">μέν-ω</span> _remain_, <span lang="grc">μενῶ, ἔμεινα; στέλλω</span> (<span lang="grc">στελ-</span>) _send_, <span lang="grc">στελῶ, ἔστειλα</span>. Here belong verbs in <span lang="grc">-ελλω, -εμω, -εμνω, -ερω, -ειρω, -ενω, -εινω</span>.

**c. <span lang="grc">ῐ</span>** in the future, <span lang="grc"> **ῑ** </span> in the aorist: <span lang="grc">κλί̄νω</span> (<span lang="grc">κλιν-</span>) _incline_, <span lang="grc">κλῐνῶ, ἔκλῑνα</span>. Here belong verbs in <span lang="grc">-ιλλω, -ῑνω, -ῑρω</span>.

**d. <span lang="grc">ῠ</span>** in the future, <span lang="grc"> **ῡ** </span> in the aorist: <span lang="grc">σύ̄ρω</span> (<span lang="grc">συρ-</span>) _drag_, <span lang="grc">σῠρῶ, ἔσῡρα</span>. Here belong verbs in <span lang="grc">-ῡρω, -ῡνω</span>.

For the formation of the future stem see [535](03-ii-future-system.md#s535), of the aorist stem see [544](04-iii-first-sigmatic-aorist-system.md#s544).

##### <a id="s616"></a> §616

For the perfect stem see [559](06-v-first-k-perfect-system.md#s559). Few liquid verbs make second perfects. On the change of <span lang="grc">ε, α</span> of the verb-stem to <span lang="grc">ο, η</span> in the second perfect, see [478](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s478), [484](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s484).

Liquid verbs with futures in <span lang="grc">-ῶ</span> do not form future perfects.

##### <a id="s617"></a> §617

Monosyllabic verb-stems containing <span lang="grc">ε</span> have <span lang="grc">α</span> in the first perfect active, perfect middle, first aorist and future passive and in all second aorists, but <span lang="grc">ο</span> in the second perfect. Thus, <span lang="grc">φθείρω</span> (<span lang="grc">φθερ-</span>) _corrupt_, <span lang="grc">ἔφθαρκα, ἔφθαρμαι, ἐφθάρην</span>, but <span lang="grc">δι-έφθορα</span> _have destroyed_ ([819](16-irregular-mi-verbs.md#s819)).

##### <a id="s618"></a> §618

A few monosyllabic stems do not change <span lang="grc">ε</span> to <span lang="grc">α</span> in the 2 aor., as <span lang="grc">τέμνω</span> _cut_ <span lang="grc">ἔτεμον</span> (but <span lang="grc">ἔταμον</span> in Hom., Hdt. etc.), <span lang="grc">γίγνομαι</span> (<span lang="grc">γεν-</span>) _become_ <span lang="grc">ἐγενόμην</span>. See also <span lang="grc">θείνω, θέρομαι, κέλομαι</span>, root <span lang="grc">φεν-</span>. Few liquid verbs form second aorists.

##### <a id="s619"></a> §619

Stems of more than one syllable do not change the vowel of the verb-stem.

##### <a id="s621"></a> §621

**Stop Verbs**.—Many verb stems end in a stop (or mute) consonant.

The present is formed either from the simple verb-stem, as in <span lang="grc">πλέκ-ω</span> _weave_, or by the addition of <span lang="grc">τ</span> or <span lang="grc">\[ιγλιδε\]</span> (_y_) to the verb-stem, as in <span lang="grc">βλάπτω</span> (<span lang="grc">βλαβ-</span>) _injure_, <span lang="grc">φυλάττω</span> (<span lang="grc">φυλακ-\[ιγλιδε\]ω</span>) _guard_. All tenses except the present and imperfect are formed without the addition of <span lang="grc">τ</span> or <span lang="grc">ῖ</span> to the verb-stem; thus, <span lang="grc">βλάψω</span> from <span lang="grc">βλαβ-σ-ω, φυλάξω</span> from <span lang="grc">φυλακ-σ-ω</span>.

##### <a id="s622"></a> §622

Some monosyllabic stems show a variation in the quantity of the stem vowel <span lang="grc">ι</span> or <span lang="grc">υ</span>, as <span lang="grc">τρί̄βω</span> _rub_ perf. <span lang="grc">τέτρῐφα, ψύ̄χω</span> _cool_ 2 aor. pass. <span lang="grc">ἐψύ̆χην, τήκω</span> _melt_ (Doric <span lang="grc">τά̄κω</span>) 2 aor. pass. <span lang="grc">ἐτά̆κην</span>. Cp. [475](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s475), [477](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s477) c, 500. Many monosyllabic stems show qualitative vowel gradation: <span lang="grc">**ι ει οι**; **υ ευ ου**; **α η ω**; **α ε ο**</span>. For examples see [477](../09-verbs/09-formation-of-the-tense-systems-omega-and-mi-verbs.md#s477)-484.

##### <a id="s623"></a> §623
