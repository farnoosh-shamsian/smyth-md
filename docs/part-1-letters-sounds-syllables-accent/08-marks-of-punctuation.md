# MARKS OF PUNCTUATION

##### <a id="s188"></a> §188

Greek has four marks of punctuation. The _comma_ and _period_ have the same forms as in English. For the _colon_ and _semicolon_ Greek has only one sign, a point above the line (.): <span lang="grc">οἱ δὲ ἡδέως ἐπείθοντο· ἐπίστευον γὰρ αὐτῷ</span> _and they gladly obeyed; for they trusted him_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 1.2.2">X. A. 1.2.2</cite>. The _mark of interrogation_ (;) is the same as our semicolon: <span lang="grc">πῶς γὰρ οὔ</span>; _for why not?_
