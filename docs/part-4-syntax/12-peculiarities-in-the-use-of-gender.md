# PECULIARITIES IN THE USE OF GENDER

##### <a id="s1013"></a> §1013

**Construction according to the Sense** ([926 a](05-agreement-the-concords.md#s926)).—The real, not the grammatical, gender often determines the agreement: <span lang="grc">ὦ φίλτατ᾽, ὦ περισσὰ τῑμηθεὶς τέκνον</span> _O dearest_, _O greatly honoured child_ <cite data-ref="Eur. Tro. 735">E. Tro. 735</cite> (this use of the attributive adjective is poetical), <span lang="grc">τὰ μειράκια πρὸς ἀλλήλους διαλεγόμενοι</span> _the youths conversing with one another_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Lach. 180e">P. Lach. 180e</cite>, <span lang="grc">ταῦτ᾽ ἔλεγεν ἡ ἀναιδὴς αὕτη κεφαλή, ἐξεληλυθώς</span> _this shameless fellow spoke thus when he came out_ <cite data-ref="Dem. 21.117">D. 21.117</cite>.

##### <a id="s1014"></a> §1014

So in periphrases: <span lang="grc">ἲ̄ς Τηλεμάχοιο ἐς πατέρα ἰδών</span> _mighty Telemachus_, _gazing at his father_ <cite data-ref="Hom. Od. 16.476"><span lang="grc">π</span> 476</cite>, <span lang="grc">τὸ δὲ τῶν πρεσβυτέρων ἡμῶν . . . χαίροντες τῇ ἐκείνων παιδιᾷ</span> _we the elders delighting in their sport_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Laws 657d">P. L. 657d</cite>.

##### <a id="s1015"></a> §1015

The masculine is used for person in general: <span lang="grc">οὐκ ἀνέξεται τίκτονται ἄλλους, οὐκ ἔχουσ᾽ αὐτὴ τέκνα</span> _unfruitful herself, she will not endure that others_ <!-- page 272 --> _bear children_ <cite data-ref="Eur. Andr. 712">E. And. 712</cite>, <span lang="grc">ὁπότερος ἂν ᾖ βελτί̄ων, εἴθ᾽ ὁ ἀνήρ εἴθ᾽ ἡ γυνή</span> _which ever of the two is superior, whether the man or the woman_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Ec. 7.27">X. O. 7.27</cite>. So <span lang="grc">οἱ γονεῖς</span> _parents_, <span lang="grc">οἱ παῖδες</span> _children_. See [1055](14-adjectives.md#s1055).
