# PARTICLES: μά

##### <a id="s2894"></a> §2894

<span lang="grc"> **μά** </span> asseverative (cp. <span lang="grc">μήν, μέν</span> asseverative) with the accusative of the divinity or thing by which one swears. In negative sentences we have <span lang="grc">οὐ μά</span> or <span lang="grc">μά</span> alone with the accusative; in affirmative sentences, <span lang="grc">ναὶ μά</span>, but more commonly <span lang="grc">νή</span>. The omission of the accusative may sometimes be due to indecision or to indifference and not always to scrupulousness ([1596 c](../18-the-cases/07-accusative.md#s1596)). <span lang="grc">μά</span> means properly _in truth_, _verily;_ but apparently governs the accusative after the ellipse of such verbs as _I call to witness_.
