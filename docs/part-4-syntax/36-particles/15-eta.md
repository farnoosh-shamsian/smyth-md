# PARTICLES: ἦ

##### <a id="s2864"></a> §2864

**Asseverative <span lang="grc">ἦ</span>** (prepositive) _in truth, in sooth, verily_, _upon my honour_, etc.; as <span lang="grc">ἦ καλῶς λέγεις</span> <cite data-ref="Plat. Gorg. 447c">P. G. 447c</cite>.

##### <a id="s2865"></a> §2865

<span lang="grc">ἦ</span> is usually associated with other particles.

<span lang="grc"> **ἦ γάρ** </span> when used alone in dialogue = _is it not so?_ Cp. _n'est ce pas, nicht wahr?_ Elsewhere it often has the force of _am I to understand that_ asked with surprise. Thus, <span lang="grc">ἦ γὰρ νοεῖς θάπτειν σφ᾽, ἀπόρρητον πόλει</span>; _what_, _dost_ <!-- page 650 --> _thou in truth intend to bury him, when it is forbidden to the citizens?_ <cite data-ref="Soph. Ant. 44">S. Ant. 44</cite>.

<span lang="grc"> **ἦ δή** </span> expresses lively surprise.

<span lang="grc"> **ἦ καί** </span> is found in animated questions. Here <span lang="grc">καί</span> goes closely with <span lang="grc">ἦ</span>.

<span lang="grc"> **ἦ μήν** </span> (Hom. <span lang="grc">ἦ μέν, ἦ μά̄ν</span>) prefaces strong asseverations, threats, and oaths, in direct and indirect discourse. Thus, <span lang="grc">ἦ μὴν ἐγὼ ἔπαθόν τι τοιοῦτον</span> _in truth this was my experience_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Apol. 22a">P. A. 22a</cite>, <span lang="grc">ὄμνῡμι θεοὺς . . . ἦ μὴν μήτε με Ξενοφῶντα κελεῦσαι ἀφελέσθαι τὸν ἄνδρα μήτε ἄλλον ὑ̄μῶν μηδένα</span> _I swear by the gods upon my honour neither did Xenophon nor any one else among you bid me rescue the man_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 6.6.17">X. A. 6.6.17</cite>.

<span lang="grc"> **ἦ που** </span> _indeed_, _methinks_, in poetry _I ween_. Here the shade of doubt indicated by <span lang="grc">πού</span> is not real.

##### <a id="s2866"></a> §2866

**Interrogative <span lang="grc">ἦ</span>** ([2650](../32-interrogative-sentences-questions.md#s2650)) is probably the same as asseverative <span lang="grc">ἦ</span>.
