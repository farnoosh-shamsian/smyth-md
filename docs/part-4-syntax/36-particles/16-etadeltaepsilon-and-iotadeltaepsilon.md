# PARTICLES: ἠδέ (AND ἰδε)

##### <a id="s2867"></a> §2867

<span lang="grc"> **ἠδέ** </span> _and_ (Epic, lyric, tragic); also in conjunction with <span lang="grc">τε καί</span>, or <span lang="grc">δέ. ἠμέν . . . ἠδέ</span> (Epic) is used like <span lang="grc">τὲ . . . τέ, καὶ . . . καί</span>.

<span lang="grc"> **ἰδέ** </span> _and_ (Epic, rare in tragedy) is used where <span lang="grc">ἠδέ</span> does not suit the metre.
