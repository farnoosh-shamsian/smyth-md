# PARTICLES: γοῦν

##### <a id="s2830"></a> §2830

<span lang="grc"> **γοῦν** </span> (postpositive; first in Aeschylus) is a restrictive particle from <span lang="grc">γέ ¨ οὖν</span>. Its meaning varies according to the prominence of the <span lang="grc">γέ</span> or <span lang="grc">οὖν</span>; often _certainly_, _at any rate_ (_at all events, at least_). <span lang="grc">γοῦν</span> commonly confirms a previous general assertion by giving a special instance of its truth (the special instance may be a seeming exception). <span lang="grc">γοῦν</span> is thus used in bringing forward a reason, which, while not absolutely conclusive, is the most probable explanation of a previous statement.

<span lang="grc">ἔτι γὰρ οὗτοι κακί̄ονές εἰσι τῶν ὑφ᾽ ἡμῶν ἡττημένων· ἔφευγον γοῦν πρὸς ἐκείνους καταλιπόντες ἡμᾶς</span> _for they are even more cowardly than those who were beaten by us. At any rate they deserted us and sought refuge with them_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 3.2.17">X. A. 3.2.17</cite>. <!-- page 644 -->

##### <a id="s2831"></a> §2831

<span lang="grc">γοῦν</span> may emphasize a pronoun; as <span lang="grc">πρὸς γοῦν ἐμοῦ</span> <cite data-ref="Soph. Aj. 527">S. Aj. 527</cite>, <span lang="grc">τὰ γοῦν σά</span> <cite data-ref="Soph. El. 1499">S. El. 1499</cite>.

##### <a id="s2832"></a> §2832

In answers <span lang="grc">γοῦν</span> means _well_, _at least; yes certainly;_ as <span lang="grc">εἰκὸς γοῦν</span> <cite data-ref="Xen. Cyrop. 5.3.14">X. C. 5.3.14</cite>.

##### <a id="s2833"></a> §2833

<span lang="grc">γοῦν</span> finds the proof of an assertion in _one_ of several possible facts or occurrences; <span lang="grc">γάρ</span> gives the reason in general, but gives no particular instance; <span lang="grc">δ᾽ οὖν</span> has an adversative force: ‘be that as it may, _yet at any rate_.’
