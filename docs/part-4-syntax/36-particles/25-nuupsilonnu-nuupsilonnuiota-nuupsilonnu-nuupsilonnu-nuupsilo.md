# PARTICLES: νῦν, νῡνί̄, νύ̄ν, νύν, νύ

##### <a id="s2924"></a> §2924

<span lang="grc">νῦν</span> _now_, _at present_ often has a causal sense, as <span lang="grc">νῦν δέ</span> _but as the case stands_, _as it is;_ often to mark reality in contrast to an assumed case.

##### <a id="s2925"></a> §2925

<span lang="grc">νῡνί̄</span> (<span lang="grc">νῦν</span> + deictic <span lang="grc">ῑ</span>, 333 g) is stronger than <span lang="grc">νῦν</span>: _even now, at this moment;_ rarely in a causal sense.

##### <a id="s2926"></a> §2926

<span lang="grc"> **νύ̆ν** </span> (enclitic; lyric, tragic, Herodotus, rare and suspected in Homer), a weakened form of <span lang="grc">νῦν</span>, is rarely temporal, usually inferential, as _now_ is used for _then_, _therefore_. <span lang="grc">νύν</span> thus marks the connection of the speaker's thought with the situation in which he is placed. It is commonly used after imperatives, prohibitive and hortatory subjunctives. Thus, <span lang="grc">κάθιζε νύν με</span> _seat me, then_ <cite data-ref="Soph. OC 21">S. O. C. 21</cite>. In Xenophon and Plato <span lang="grc">νυν</span> is written by some editors, where the Mss. have <span lang="grc">νῦν</span> (<cite data-ref="Xen. Cyrop. 4.2.37">X. C. 4.2.37</cite>, H. 4. 1. [39](../../part-1-letters-sounds-syllables-accent/01-the-letters.md#s39)).

##### <a id="s2927"></a> §2927

<span lang="grc"> **νύ̄ν** </span> (enclitic) is adopted by some scholars in Attic tragedy where a long syllable is required (<cite data-ref="Soph. OT 644">S. O. T. 644</cite>). Others write <span lang="grc">νῦν</span> (with the _force_ of <span lang="grc">νύ̆ν</span>).

##### <a id="s2928"></a> §2928

<span lang="grc"> **νύ̆** </span> (enclitic; Epic and Cyprian), a still weaker form of <span lang="grc">νῦν</span>, and less emphatic than <span lang="grc">δή</span>. It is common in questions and appeals; less frequent in statements; as <span lang="grc">τίς νυ</span>; _who now?_ Also after other particles, as <span lang="grc">καί νύ κε, ἦ ῥά νυ</span>. <!-- page 660 -->
