# PARTICLES: δαί, δῆθεν, δήπου, δῆτα

##### <a id="s2848"></a> §2848

<span lang="grc"> **δαί** </span> is used in colloquial Attic after interrogative words to express wonder, indignation, etc. Thus, <span lang="grc">τί δαί; πῶς δαί</span>; _what then? how so?_

##### <a id="s2849"></a> §2849

<span lang="grc"> **δῆθεν** </span> _truly_, _forsooth_, is commonly used of apparent or pretended truth, and mostly with an ironical tone. Thus, <span lang="grc">ἐκερτόμησας δῆθεν ὡς παῖδ᾽ ὄντα με</span> _thou hast mocked me forsooth as though I were a child_ <cite data-ref="Aesch. PB 986">A. Pr. 986</cite>.

##### <a id="s2850"></a> §2850

<span lang="grc"> **δήπου** </span> _probably_, _I presume_, _I should hope, doubtless_, _you will admit_, is stronger than <span lang="grc">πού</span> _perhaps_, _I suppose_. <span lang="grc">δήπου</span> often has a touch of irony or doubt in stating a case that would seem to be certain; as <span lang="grc">ἴστε δήπου ὅθεν ἥλιος ἀνίσχει</span> _you know_, _I presume_, _where the sun rises_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 5.7.6">X. A. 5.7.6</cite>. In questions <span lang="grc">δήπου</span> expects the answer _yes_. <span lang="grc">οὐ δήπου</span> _certainly not_ and _is it not so?_ (with irony).

##### <a id="s2851"></a> §2851

<span lang="grc"> **δῆτα** </span> _assuredly_, _really_, _in truth_, is rare outside of Attic. It occurs: (a) In answers, often when a word is repeated with assent; as <span lang="grc">γιγνώσκεθ᾽ ὑ̄μεῖς ἥτις ἔσθ᾽ ἥδ᾽ ἡ γυνή; γιγνώσκομεν δῆτα</span> _do you know who this woman is? Yes indeed we do_ <cite data-ref="Aristoph. Thes. 606">Ar. Thesm. 606</cite>; <span lang="grc">οὐ δῆτα</span> _surely not_, in strong or indignant denial. (b) In questions, to mark an inference or consequence, as <span lang="grc">πῶς δῆτα</span>; _how in truth?_ <span lang="grc">τί δῆτα</span>; _what then?_ <span lang="grc">καὶ δῆτα ἐτόλμας</span>; _and didst thou really dare?_ <cite data-ref="Soph. Ant. 449">S. Ant. 449</cite>. (c) In wishes and deprecations (stronger than <span lang="grc">δή</span>), as <span lang="grc">σκόπει δῆτα</span> _only look_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Gorg. 452b">P. G. 452b</cite>, <span lang="grc">μὴ δῆτα, θῡμέ, μή σύ γ᾽ ἐργάσῃ τάδε</span> _no indeed, my heart_, _do not this deed_ <cite data-ref="Eur. Med. 1056">E. Med. 1056</cite>.
