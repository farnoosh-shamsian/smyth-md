# PARTICLES: ἆρα

##### <a id="s2800"></a> §2800

<span lang="grc"> **ἆρα** </span>, a confirmative particle from <span lang="grc">ἦ ¨ ἄρα</span>, is used in lyric and dramatic poetry in the sense of <span lang="grc">ἄρα. ἆρα</span> is postpositive, except in New Comedy.

<span lang="grc">σὸν ἆρα τοὔργον, οὐκ ἐμὸν κεκλήσεται</span> _it shall then be called thy work, not mine_ <cite data-ref="Soph. Aj. 1368">S. Aj. 1368</cite>. Often with <span lang="grc">τίς</span>, as <span lang="grc">τίς ἆρ᾽ ἐμοῦ γένοιτ᾽ ἂν ἀ̈θλιώτερος</span>; _who then could be more wretched than I am?_ Trag. fr. 280. On interrogative <span lang="grc">ἆρα</span>, see [2650](../32-interrogative-sentences-questions.md#s2650), [2651](../32-interrogative-sentences-questions.md#s2651). Epic <span lang="grc"> **ἦ ῥα** </span> is both confirmatory and interrogative.
