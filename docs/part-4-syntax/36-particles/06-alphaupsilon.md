# PARTICLES: αὖ

##### <a id="s2802"></a> §2802

<span lang="grc"> **αὖ** </span> (postpositive), an adversative particle meaning _on the other hand, on the contrary_ (properly _again_). In Hom. it serves as a correlative to <span lang="grc">μέν</span> or <span lang="grc">ἦ τοι</span>, and to introduce the apodosis of conditional or relative clauses.

<span lang="grc">αὖ</span> is often used with personal pronouns, as <span lang="grc">ἀλλὰ σὺ αὖ . . . λέγε</span> _but do you in turn tell us_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Sym. 3">X. S. 3</cite>. 5; and is often added to <span lang="grc">δέ</span>, as <span lang="grc">οἱ Ἕλληνες ἐπῇσαν . . . οἱ δ᾽ αὖ βάρβαροι οὐκ ἐδέχοντο</span> _the Greeks came on, but the barbarians on their part did not wait to receive them_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 1.10.11">X. A. 1.10.11</cite>. Connected in meaning are the derivatives <span lang="grc"> **αὖτε** </span> (poetic) and <span lang="grc"> **αὖθις** </span>.
