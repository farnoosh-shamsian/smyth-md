# PARTICLES: μέντοι

##### <a id="s2917"></a> §2917

<span lang="grc"> **μέντοι** </span> (postpositive) from <span lang="grc">μέν</span> (= <span lang="grc">μήν</span>, [2895](21-muepsilonnu.md#s2895)) + <span lang="grc">τοί</span>, is an asseverative and adversative particle.

##### <a id="s2918"></a> §2918

Asseverative <span lang="grc">μέντοι</span> _certainly_, _surely_, _of course, in truth_ is very common in replies, where it expresses positive, eager, or reflective assent. Often with <span lang="grc">νὴ</span> (<span lang="grc">μὰ</span>) <span lang="grc">Δία</span>. Thus, <span lang="grc">ἐγώ; σὺ μέντοι</span> _I? certainly_, _you_ <cite data-ref="Aristoph. Kn. 168">Ar. Eq. 168</cite>, <span lang="grc">τί γάρ, ἔφη, . . . μέμνησαι ἐκεῖνα . . . ; ναὶ μὰ Δία . . . μέμνημαι μέντοι τοιαῦτα ἀκούσᾱς σου</span> _well then, said he, do you recall those matters; Yes_, _by Zeus_, _certainly I do recall that I heard things to that effect from you_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Cyrop. 1.6.6">X. C. 1.6.6</cite>, <span lang="grc">ἀληθέστατα μέντοι λέγεις</span> _well_, _certainly you say what is very true_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Soph. 245b">P. Soph. 245b</cite>.

<span lang="grc">μέντοι</span> may strengthen asseverations or emphasize questions; as <span lang="grc">οὕτω μέντοι χρὴ λέγειν</span> _in truth we must speak thus_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Theaet. 187b">P. Th. 187b</cite>; often with demonstrative pronouns, as <span lang="grc">ὦ τοῦτο μέντοι νὴ Δία αὐτοῖσιν πιθοῦ</span> _oh_, _by Zeus do oblige them in_ **this** Ar. Aves 661.

**a.** Asseverative <span lang="grc">μέντοι</span> in combinations, _e.g.:_

<span lang="grc"> **ἀλλὰ μέντοι** </span> _but surely, but in fact_ (in <span lang="grc">ἀλλὰ . . . μέντοι, μέντοι</span> refers to the preceding word).

<span lang="grc"> **καὶ . . . μέντοι** </span> _and . . . indeed_, _and . . . in fact, and . . . moreover_, as <span lang="grc">φιλοθηρότατος ἦν καὶ πρὸς τὰ θηρία μέντοι φιλοκινδῡνότατος</span> _he was very fond of hunting and moreover exceedingly fond of danger_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 1.9.6">X. A. 1.9.6</cite>.

<span lang="grc"> **ου᾽ μέντοι** </span> _no indeed_ (also adversative: _yet not_).

##### <a id="s2919"></a> §2919

Adversative <span lang="grc">μέντοι</span> _however_, _yet_ often marks a contrast or a transition; as <span lang="grc">ἀφί̄εμέν σε, ἐπὶ τούτῳ μέντοι</span> _we let you go, on this condition however_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Apol. 29c">P. A. 29c</cite>. <span lang="grc">μέντοι γε</span> is stronger. <span lang="grc"> **μὲν . . . μέντοι** </span> is much stronger than <span lang="grc">μὲν . . . δέ</span>, as <span lang="grc">φιλοσόφῳ μὲν ἔοικας . . . ἴσθι μέντοι ἀνόητος ὤν</span> _you resemble a philosopher— know however that you are a fool_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 2.1.13">X. A. 2.1.13</cite>. On <span lang="grc"> **ου᾽ μέντοι ἀλλά** </span> (<span lang="grc"> **γε** </span>) see [2767](../35-negative-sentences.md#s2767).
