# PARTICLES: πέρ

##### <a id="s2965"></a> §2965

<span lang="grc"> **πέρ** </span> (postpositive and enclitic) _very_, _just_, _even_. Cp. Epic <span lang="grc">πέρι</span> _very much_, and <span lang="grc">περί</span> in composition. In Attic prose <span lang="grc">πέρ</span> is common only with relatives ([338 c](../../part-2-inflection/06-pronouns.md#s338)) and conjunctions. <!-- page 666 -->

<span lang="grc">ὅσπερ</span> _the very one who_ (i.e. _none other_), <span lang="grc">οἷός περ</span> _just such_, <span lang="grc">ἔνθα περ</span> _just where_, <span lang="grc">ὥσπερ</span> _just as, in the very way in which_, (sometimes not very different from <span lang="grc">ὡς</span>, to which it is related as <span lang="grc">ὅσπερ</span> to <span lang="grc">ὅς</span>), <span lang="grc">εἴπερ</span> _if really_. <span lang="grc">καίπερ</span> (Hom. <span lang="grc">καὶ . . . περ</span>) _however much, though_, Epic <span lang="grc">ἠέ περ</span> _just as_.

**a.** After other words especially in Epic and Lyric and in Aeschylus; as <span lang="grc">μένει τὸ θεῖον δουλίᾳ περ έν φρενί</span> _the divine power remains in the mind though it be enslaved_ <cite data-ref="Aesch. Ag. 1084">A. Ag. 1084</cite>, <span lang="grc">μάχετ᾽, ἀχνύμενός περ ἑταίρου</span> _he fought_, (_though_) _sore grieving for his comrade_ P 459, <span lang="grc">ὀψέ περ</span> _howbeit late_ Pind. Nem. 3. 80.
