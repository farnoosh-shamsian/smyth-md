# PARTICLES: πλήν

##### <a id="s2966"></a> §2966

<span lang="grc"> **πλήν** </span> an adverb, is used (_a_) as a preposition with the genitive ([1700](../19-prepositions.md#s1700)) meaning _except_, _save_, when that which is excepted is a single substantival idea; (_b_) as a conjunction, _except_, _except that, save that_, _unless_, _only_, _but_ (often almost = <span lang="grc">ἀλλά</span>).

<span lang="grc">ἀφειστήκεσαν . . . πᾶσαι πλὴν Μῑλήτου</span> _all_ the Ionic cities _had revolted except Miletus_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 1.1.6">X. A. 1.1.6</cite>; <span lang="grc">οὐδεὶς ἀπῄει πρὸς βασιλέᾱ, πλὴν Ὀρόντᾱς ἐπεχείρησε</span> _no one went off to the king save that Orontas made the attempt_ 1. 9. 29, <span lang="grc">πλὴν ἓν μόνον δέδοικα</span> _but there is one thing and only one that I fear_ <cite data-ref="Aristoph. Pl. 199">Ar. Plut. 199</cite>. A substantive-equivalent may follow <span lang="grc">πλήν</span>, not in the genitive, but in the case required by the verb of the sentence, as <span lang="grc">συνῆλθον πάντες πλὴν οἱ Νέωνος</span> _all assembled except the men under Neon_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Anab. 7.3.2">X. A. 7.3.2</cite>.

**a.** <span lang="grc"> **πλὴν οὐ** </span> _only not, except_ ([2753](../35-negative-sentences.md#s2753)); <span lang="grc"> **πλὴν ἤ** </span> _except_, as <span lang="grc">οὐ γὰρ ἄλλῳ γ᾽ ὑπακούσαιμεν . . . πλὴν ἢ Προδίκῳ</span> _we would not listen to any one_ (_else_) _except Prodicus_ <cite data-ref="Aristoph. Cl. 361">Ar. Nub. 361</cite>; <span lang="grc"> **πλὴν ὅτι** </span> _except that;_ <span lang="grc"> **πλὴν εἰ** </span> _except if_, cp. <span lang="grc">εἰ μή</span> (_nisi si_), after a negative <span lang="grc">πλὴν εἰ μή</span>; often with the verb omitted, as <span lang="grc">οὐδεὶς οἶδεν . . . πλὴν εἴ τις ἄρ᾽ ὄρνις</span> _no one knows except perhaps some bird_ <cite data-ref="Aristoph. Birds 601">Ar. Av. 601</cite>.

**b.** <span lang="grc">πλήν</span> may be followed by the infinitive, as <span lang="grc">τί σοι πέπρᾱκται πρᾶγμα πλὴν τεύχειν κακά</span>; _what hast thou accomplished save to work mischief?_ <cite data-ref="Aesch. Eum. 125">A. Eum. 125</cite>.
