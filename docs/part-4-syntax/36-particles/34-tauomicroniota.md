# PARTICLES: τοί

##### <a id="s2984"></a> §2984

<span lang="grc"> **τοί** </span> (postpositive and enclitic) _in truth, surely_, _doubtless_, _mark you_, _be assured, you_ (_must_) _know_, was originally the dative of feeling ([1486](../18-the-cases/06-dative.md#s1486)) of <span lang="grc">σύ</span>.

**a.** This <span lang="grc">τοί</span> (Sanskrit _tē_), found in all dialects, is to be distinguished from Doric <span lang="grc">τοί</span> ( = <span lang="grc">σοί</span>) from <span lang="grc">τνοι</span> (Skt. _tvē´_). <span lang="grc">τοί</span> may thus occur in the same sentence with <span lang="grc">σοί</span>; as <span lang="grc">τοιαῦτά τοί σοι . . . λέγω</span> S. fr. 25.

##### <a id="s2985"></a> §2985

<span lang="grc">τοί</span> is often used in statements of a general truth and in expressions of personal conviction (sometimes with a tone of hesitation); in remarks of a confidential nature; to introduce an explanation; and in general where the special attention of the person addressed is desired. <span lang="grc">τοί</span> often gives an easy and familiar tone to a reply. Thus, <span lang="grc">τῶν τοι ματαίων ἀνδράσιν φρονημάτων ἡ γλῶσσ᾽ ἀληθὴς γίγνεται κατήγορος</span> _true it is that of men's vain conceits their tongue is the true accuser_ <cite data-ref="Aesch. Seven 438">A. Sept. 438</cite>, <span lang="grc">ἀεί τοι ὁ Κέβης λόγους τινὰς ἀνερευνᾷ</span> _for Cebes_, _you know_, _is always investigating some speculation or other_ <cite data-ref="Plat. Phaedo 63a">P. Ph. 63a</cite>.

**a.** <span lang="grc">τοί</span> may emphasize particular words, as <span lang="grc">ἐγώ τοι, ἐμοί τοι, σέ τοι</span>; and other words not pronouns.

##### <a id="s2986"></a> §2986

<span lang="grc">τοί</span> is frequently used after other particles, as <span lang="grc">ἀλλά, γάρ, γέ, δή</span> (and <span lang="grc">γέ τοι δή</span>, cp. <span lang="grc">δή τοι . . . γε</span>), <span lang="grc">ἐπεί</span> _because_, <span lang="grc">μή, οὐ</span> (<span lang="grc">οὔτοι</span>). On <span lang="grc">ἤτοι</span>, see [2858](14-eta.md#s2858); on <span lang="grc">καίτοι</span>, 2893; on <span lang="grc">μέντοι</span>, 2917.

##### <a id="s2987"></a> §2987

The inferential conjunctions <span lang="grc">τοιγάρ, τοιγαροῦν, τοιγάρτοι, τοίνυν</span> contain <span lang="grc">τοί</span>, the locative of the demonstrative <span lang="grc">τό</span>, which case had the meaning of <span lang="grc">τῷ</span> (<span lang="grc">τῶ</span>) _therefore_, _on this account, so_ lit. _by that_, _therein_. (This <span lang="grc">τῷ</span> is chiefly Epic, and stands at the beginning of the verse. Cp. <span lang="grc">τό</span> _therefore_ <cite data-ref="Hom. Il. 3.176"><span lang="grc">Γ</span> 176</cite>, <cite data-ref="Soph. Phil. 142">S. Ph. 142</cite>.)

<span lang="grc"> **τοιγάρ** </span> (prepositive; Ionic and poetic) _therefore_, _wherefore_, _so then, that is surely the reason why_ (often to announce a purpose).

<span lang="grc">**τοιγαροῦν**, **τοιγάρτοι**</span> (both prepositive) are more emphatic than <span lang="grc">τοιγάρ</span>. The final syllable of <span lang="grc">τοιγάρτοι</span> is the <span lang="grc">τοί</span> of 2984.

<span lang="grc"> **τοίνυν** </span> (postpositive and post-Homeric; <span lang="grc">-νυν</span> [2927](25-nuupsilonnu-nuupsilonnuiota-nuupsilonnu-nuupsilonnu-nuupsilo.md#s2927)) is transitional (_now then, further_) or inferential (_therefore_, _accordingly;_ less emphatic than <span lang="grc">τοιγάρ</span>). <span lang="grc">τοι&lt;\*&gt;νυν</span> is common when a speaker refers to something present in his mind, when <!-- page 670 --> he continues or resumes what he has been saying, and when he passes to a new aspect of a subject. It is often found with imperatives (<span lang="grc">σκόπει τοίνυν</span> <cite data-ref="Plat. Crito 51c">P. Cr. 51c</cite>).
