# THE SUBJECT

##### <a id="s927"></a> §927

The subject of a finite verb is in the nominative: <span lang="grc">Κῦρος ἐβόᾱ</span> _Cyrus called out_.

##### <a id="s928"></a> §928

The subject nominative may be replaced

**a.** By a prepositional phrase in the accusative: <span lang="grc">ἐνθῡμεῖσθε καθ᾽ ἑκάστους τε καὶ ξύμπαντες</span> _consider individually and all together_ <cite data-ref="Thuc. 7.64">T. 7.64</cite>.

**b.** By a genitive of the divided whole ([1318](18-the-cases/03-genitive.md#s1318)): <span lang="grc">Πελληνεῖς δὲ κατὰ Θεσπιέᾱς γενόμενοι ἐμάχοντό τε καὶ ἐν χώρᾳ ἔπῑπτον ἑκατέρων</span> _the Pellenians who were opposed to the Thespians kept up the contest and several on both sides fell on the spot_ <cite data-ref="Xen. Hell. 4.2.20">X. H. 4.2.20</cite>.
