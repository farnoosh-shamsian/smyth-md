# SUMMARY OF THE FORMS OF SIMPLE SENTENCES

<!-- page 481 -->

§§ 906-2152 deal, in general, with the simple sentence. The following summary shows the chief forms of simple sentences ([921](03-kinds-of-simple-sentences.md#s921)) used in Attic.

##### <a id="s2153"></a> §2153

**STATEMENTS**

\1. Statements of Fact (direct assertions) as to the present, past, or future are made in the indicative mood (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>), 1770.

A. Statements of fact include statements of present, past, or future possibility, likelihood, or necessity, which are expressed by the indicative of a verb denoting possibility, likelihood, or necessity, and an infinitive ([1774](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1774)-[1779](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1779)).

B. Statements of customary or repeated past action are made in the imperfect or aorist indicative with <span lang="grc">ἄν</span> (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>), 1790.

\2. Statement of Opinion (usually cautious, doubtful, or modest assertions) as to what _may be_ (_might be_), _can be_ (_could be_), _may_ (_might_, _could_, _would_) _have been_, etc., are made:

A. In reference to the present or past: by <span lang="grc">ἐβουλόμην ἄν</span> _I should like_ or _I should have liked_ (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>), 1789. (Rarely by the indicative without <span lang="grc">ἄν</span>, negative <span lang="grc">μή</span> or <span lang="grc">μὴ οὐ</span>, 1772.)

B. In reference to the past: by the aorist or imperfect indicative with <span lang="grc">ἄν</span> (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>), 1784, cp. [1786](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1786).

C. In reference to the present (statement of present opinion the verification of which is left to the future): by the optative with <span lang="grc">ἄν</span> (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>), 1824.

D. In reference to the future: by the present subjunctive with <span lang="grc">μή</span> or <span lang="grc">μὴ οὐ</span> ([1801](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1801)); by <span lang="grc">οὐ μή</span> with the aorist subjunctive to denote an emphatic denial ([1804](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1804)).

##### <a id="s2154"></a> §2154

**ASSUMPTIONS**

Assumptions, including concessions, are usually expressed by the imperative (negative <span lang="grc">μή</span>), 1839. Other forms occur, as <span lang="grc">καὶ δή</span> with the indicative (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>), 1771; a _verb of assuming_ with the accu sative and infinitive, etc.

##### <a id="s2155"></a> §2155

**COMMANDS (INCLUDING EXHORTATIONS)**

\1. **Positive** Commands are expressed by the

A. Imperative, except in the first person ([1835](20-the-verb-voices/08-imperative.md#s1835)).

B. Subjunctive, in the first person ([1797](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1797)).

C. Future indicative (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>) 1917, 1918; with <span lang="grc">ὅπως</span> ([1920](20-the-verb-voices/10-the-tenses.md#s1920)). <!-- page 482 -->

D. Optative without <span lang="grc">ἄν</span> ([1820](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1820)); potential optative with <span lang="grc">ἄν</span> (negative <span lang="grc">οὐ</span>, [1830](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1830)).

E. Infinitive used independently ([2013](21-verbal-nouns.md#s2013)).

\2. **Negative** Commands (Prohibitions, [1840](20-the-verb-voices/08-imperative.md#s1840)), including Exhortations, are expressed by <span lang="grc">μή</span> with the

A. Present imperative ([1840](20-the-verb-voices/08-imperative.md#s1840)) or aorist subjunctive (second or third person), 1800.

B. Present or aorist subjunctive in the first person plural ([1840](20-the-verb-voices/08-imperative.md#s1840)).

C. Aorist imperative in the third person (rare), 1840.

D. Future indicative with <span lang="grc">ὅπως μή</span> ([1920](20-the-verb-voices/10-the-tenses.md#s1920)); with <span lang="grc">οὐ μή</span> ([1919](20-the-verb-voices/10-the-tenses.md#s1919)).

E. Aorist subjunctive with <span lang="grc">ὅπως μή</span> (rare), 1803; with <span lang="grc">οὐ μή</span> (rare), 1800, N.

F. Infinitive used independently ([2013](21-verbal-nouns.md#s2013)).

##### <a id="s2156"></a> §2156

**WISHES**

\1. <span lang="grc">μή</span> is the negative of a direct expression of a wish, and of all indirect expressions of wish except <span lang="grc">πῶς ἄν</span> with the optative and a form of <span lang="grc">βούλομαι</span> with the infinitive.

\2. Wishes for the _future_, whether the object of the wish is reasonable or unreasonable, attainable or unattainable, are expressed by the optative with or without <span lang="grc">εἴθε</span> or <span lang="grc">εἰ γάρ</span> ([1814](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1814), [1815](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1815)). Indirect expressions are: <span lang="grc">πῶς ἄν</span> with the optative ([1832](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1832)); <span lang="grc">βουλοίμην ἄν</span> with the infinitive ([1827](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1827)).

\3. Wishes for the _present:_ that something might be otherwise than it now is, are expressed by the imperfect with <span lang="grc">εἴθε</span> or <span lang="grc">εἰ γάρ</span> ([1780](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1780)). Indirect expressions are: <span lang="grc">ὤφελον</span> (with or without <span lang="grc">εἴθε</span> or <span lang="grc">εἰ γάρ</span>) and the present or aorist infinitive ([1781](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1781)); <span lang="grc">ἐβουλόμην</span> (with or without <span lang="grc">ἄν</span>) with the infinitive ([1782](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1782), [1789](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1789)).

\4. Wishes for the _past:_ that something might have been otherwise than it then was, are expressed by the aorist indicative with <span lang="grc">εἴθε</span> or <span lang="grc">εἰ γάρ</span> ([1780](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1780)). Indirect: <span lang="grc">ὤφελον</span> (with or without <span lang="grc">εἴθε</span> or <span lang="grc">εἰ γάρ</span>) with the present or aorist infinitive ([1781](20-the-verb-voices/07-the-moods-in-simple-sentences.md#s1781)).

\5. Unattainable wishes for the present or past may be entirely reasonable.

##### <a id="s2157"></a> §2157

**QUESTIONS**

A simple question results from making any form of statement interrogative. Direct and indirect questions are treated in 2636 ff. See also the Index.

##### <a id="s2158"></a> §2158

**EXCLAMATIONS**

Exclamations form complete or incomplete ([904](01-definitions.md#s904)) sentences. Direct and indirect exclamatory sentences are treated in 2681 ff. See also the Index.
