# KINDS OF SIMPLE SENTENCES

<!-- page 258 -->

##### <a id="s921"></a> §921

Simple sentences have six forms: Statements; Assumptions, Commands, Wishes; Questions; and Exclamations. Of these, Assumptions, Commands, and Wishes express _will_. See [2153](24-summary-of-the-forms-of-simple-sentences.md#s2153) ff.
