# THE VERB: VOICES: THE MOODS

##### <a id="s1759"></a> §1759

Mood designates by the form of the verb the mode or manner (_modus_) in which the speaker conceives of an assertion concerning the subject.

##### <a id="s1760"></a> §1760

There are four moods proper in Greek: indicative, subjunctive, optative, and imperative. The infinitive (strictly a verbal noun) and the participle (strictly an adjective form of the verb) may be classed with the moods.
